This module runs Tax analytical checks — Income-tax analytical review, Tax Audit applicability, related-party payment screening, tax depreciation recompute, an MSME-payment review screen, and GST invoice reconciliation — against your validated, confirmed-mapped data. It never states that a tax disallowance or violation is confirmed; every finding is labeled Potential Tax Issue, Tax Review Required, Potential Disallowance — Review Required, Potential MSME Payment Review, or Insufficient Data, for a professional to review and confirm.

Act-transition scope (important): India's Income-tax Act, 1961 is being replaced by the Income-tax Act, 2025, effective 1 April 2026. Every tax rule below is verified and executable against the Income-tax Act, 1961 only — covering FY 2025-26 (Assessment Year 2026-27) and all prior years. An engagement whose financial year is FY 2026-27 or later falls under the Income-tax Act, 2025, whose provisions have not yet been independently verified — no tax rule will run for such an engagement, and this screen will show why rather than silently producing no findings.

Legal provision → FinSight analytical test → limitation → suggested query. Every rule below is shown in this order. The Provision is the verified Income-tax Act, 1961 section and its own statutory threshold, where the Act itself sets one. The FinSight Analytical Test is what FinSight actually computed from your data — the identification method is always FinSight's own, even on the rules where the rupee threshold is the Act's. No finding states a disallowance or violation is confirmed from FinSight's analytical test alone.
