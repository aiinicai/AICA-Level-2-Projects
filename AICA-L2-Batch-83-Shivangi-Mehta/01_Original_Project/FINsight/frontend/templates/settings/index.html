FinSight assists a qualified professional by surfacing potential inconsistencies, risk indicators and review points for their own judgment — it does not certify compliance, complete an audit, or replace professional review. Every finding is explicitly labeled as a potential issue or a matter requiring review, never as a confirmed conclusion.

Privacy & Data Handling

FinSight is designed for local/offline financial data processing. Client financial data is not sent to external AI or cloud services by the application. All uploaded files, findings, queries and working papers are stored locally.
