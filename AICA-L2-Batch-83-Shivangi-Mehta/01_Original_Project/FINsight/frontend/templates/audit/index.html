Audit risk indicators against your validated, confirmed-mapped data.

This module identifies audit risk indicators — unusual transactions/patterns, cut-off and journal-entry risk, related-party candidates, and estimate/judgement areas warranting attention — using standards-informed analytical checks tagged to specific audit assertions. It never turns a risk indicator into an automatic accounting exception, and never states that an audit procedure is mandatory. Findings are always labeled Audit Attention Required, Potential Audit Risk, Review Required, or Insufficient Data, for a professional to review and confirm. It runs only against data you have already validated and confirmed on the Mapping and Data Quality screens — nothing unvalidated is read, and no framework precondition applies here (audit procedures apply regardless of AS/Ind AS).

SA Reference below is the authoritative ICAI Standard on Auditing citation for the audit area a rule sits within. FinSight Analytical Test is the specific heuristic and threshold FinSight created to implement that check. The presence of an SA Reference never means the cited SA prescribes that specific threshold or test — every threshold shown (day-count windows, materiality fallbacks, percentage-movement triggers, minimum-voucher counts, ageing days, and similar) is a FinSight-configurable analytical parameter, not an SA requirement.
