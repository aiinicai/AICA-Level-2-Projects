1. Preamble

This Scope of Work Mandate (“Mandate”) is entered into between Sample Advisory Private Limited, a Mumbai based advisory firm, having its registered office at Registered office address, City 400001 (“Sample Advisory”, “the Advisor”), and {CLIENT_LEGAL}, operating under the brand {CLIENT_SHORT}, having its registered office at {CLIENT_REGISTERED_OFFICE} (“{CLIENT_SHORT}”, “the Company”).

This document sets out the objective, scope, engagement structure, process and terms under which Sample Advisory will act as Strategic Advisor and Growth Partner to {CLIENT_SHORT}. This Mandate, once signed by both parties, constitutes the governing document for the engagement, with no separate engagement letter to follow.

2. Background and Objective

{CLIENT_SHORT} is engaged in the manufacturing of building products, together with B2B trading. As the Company pursues its growth plans and expansion strategy, {CLIENT_SHORT} wishes to bring on a dedicated Strategic Advisor and Growth Partner who can work closely with the promoters across business, financial and capital raising priorities.

The objective of this Mandate is threefold:

-   Support {CLIENT_SHORT}’s growth plans through structured business advisory, strategic input and access to Sample Advisory’s network of investors, lenders and industry participants.

-   Assist the Company in raising growth capital through the most suitable structure, whether equity, strategic investment, debt, and any other route appropriate to {CLIENT_SHORT}’s requirements.

-   Build readiness across business, financial and regulatory dimensions so that {CLIENT_SHORT} is well positioned to pursue a future listing, should the Company and its promoters choose to do so.

Sample Advisory will act as {CLIENT_SHORT}’s Strategic Advisor and Growth Partner on an exclusive basis for the scope set out in this Mandate, functioning as a close, ongoing partner to the promoters and management team across strategy, fund raising and growth.

3. Scope of Services

As Strategic Advisor and Growth Partner, Sample Advisory’s mandate spans business strategy, fund raising and listing readiness together. The engagement will cover the following broad pillars, with the relative emphasis across each reviewed periodically and adjusted based on {CLIENT_SHORT}’s evolving priorities, as agreed between the parties.

  A. Business Strategy and Growth Advisory

-   Understanding {CLIENT_SHORT}’s business model, industry positioning and expansion plans, and providing ongoing strategic input to the promoters.

  B. Fund Raising and Investor Connect

-   Identifying and facilitating introductions with investors, lenders and strategic counterparties suited to {CLIENT_SHORT}’s funding requirements.

  C. Capital and Business Structuring

-   Advisory support on the most suitable capital structure and transaction structure for {CLIENT_SHORT}’s growth and fund raising plans.

  D. Governance and IPO Readiness

-   A preliminary review of governance, secretarial and financial readiness, and advisory support in preparing {CLIENT_SHORT} toward listing readiness.

  E. Transaction Coordination and Documentation

-   Coordinating with legal counsel, statutory auditors and, once appointed, a SEBI registered Lead Manager, together with preparing investor facing materials.

  F. Institutional and Board Support

-   Periodic strategic updates to the promoters and board, and general availability as a strategic sounding board through the engagement.

The precise activities within each pillar, along with priorities and sequencing, will be jointly determined with {CLIENT_SHORT}’s promoters as the engagement progresses. Sample Advisory is not registered as a Merchant Banker under applicable SEBI regulations, and accordingly will not act as a lead manager, underwriter, and any other capacity requiring such registration. Services under this Mandate are limited to advisory, coordination and facilitation, with all regulated filings undertaken by the Company and its appointed, duly registered intermediaries.

6. Engagement Model and Structure

This Mandate is structured as an exclusive, retainer based Strategic Advisory and Growth Partner engagement, reflecting the close, ongoing nature of support {CLIENT_SHORT} requires across business advisory, fund raising and readiness.

  Exclusivity          Sample Advisory is engaged on an exclusive basis for the scope set out in this Mandate during the Term

  Time Commitment      An agreed proportion of time each month, as set out in this Mandate

  Billing Cycle        Monthly retainer, payable in advance i.e. before 3^(rd) of each month, plus a success fee on funds raised (as applicable) as set out in Section 11

During the Term, {CLIENT_SHORT} agrees to route discussions with investors, lenders and strategic counterparties introduced, identified and substantially connected by Sample Advisory through Sample Advisory, so that the Company and the Advisor can work as genuine partners through to a successful outcome. The decision to accept and proceed with any investor, lender and proposal introduced by Sample Advisory remains solely with {CLIENT_SHORT}’s promoters, at their sole discretion.

7. Deliverables and Reporting Cadence

-   Periodic promoter and board level review meetings, at a frequency mutually agreed.

-   A monthly summary note covering business, fund raising and readiness progress.

-   Quarterly progress review across the scope areas set out in this Mandate.

-   Ad hoc analysis and support as required for investor meetings, negotiations and readiness decisions.

8. Roles and Responsibilities

Sample Advisory

-   Provide a designated engagement lead supported by the Sample Advisory team, under the oversight of a Director.

-   Deliver the scope of services set out in this Mandate with professional diligence and confidentiality.

-   Use its best efforts to secure the most favourable terms achievable for {CLIENT_SHORT} in any transaction facilitated under this Mandate, while recognising that no particular outcome, valuation and investor commitment can be assured.

{CLIENT_SHORT}

-   Provide timely access to business and financial records, systems and personnel required for the engagement.

-   Designate a point of contact within the Company to coordinate with the Sample Advisory engagement team.

-   Bear statutory costs, regulatory charges, legal expenses and other transaction related expenses directly and separately from the fees payable under Section 11.

9. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to treat all business, financial and investor related information exchanged in the course of this engagement as confidential, and to use such information solely for the purpose of this Mandate. This obligation will survive for two years beyond the completion, suspension and termination of the engagement.

10. Term and Termination

Term

This Mandate shall be in force from the date of acceptance by both parties and continues for three years from that date, unless extended by mutual written consent, and until brought to a close in accordance with this Section.

Termination for Convenience

Either party may bring the engagement to a close prior to expiry of the Term by providing thirty days written notice to the other party, together with a genuine reason for the closure. During the notice period, Sample Advisory will continue to deliver the agreed scope of services and will support an orderly transition of responsibilities.

Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate this Mandate with immediate effect, by written notice, if the other party commits a material breach of its obligations under this Mandate and does not remedy that breach within fifteen days of being notified in writing, and equally if the other party undergoes insolvency, winding up, and any comparable event affecting its ability to continue the engagement.

Tail Period

Where this Mandate is terminated and expires, any transaction that Sample Advisory originated and substantially progressed during the Term will continue to be covered under the success fee arrangement set out in Section 11, provided such transaction is completed within twelve months of the date of such termination and expiry.

Effect of Termination

-   Sample Advisory will be entitled to the retainer fee for the month in which termination takes effect, computed on a pro rata basis up to the effective date of termination.

-   {CLIENT_SHORT} will settle all outstanding invoices within fifteen days of the effective date of termination.

-   Sample Advisory will hand over all work in progress, reports and Company records prepared during the engagement, in a reasonably usable format.

-   Each party will return, and cease using, any confidential information and materials belonging to the other party, save for copies required to be retained for legal and regulatory record keeping purposes.

-   The confidentiality obligations under Section 9, together with the Tail Period arrangement above, will continue to apply beyond the end of this Mandate.

11. Fees and Billing

Retainer Fee

{CLIENT_SHORT} will pay Sample Advisory a monthly retainer fee of {RETAINER_FEE} plus applicable taxes, payable in advance each calendar month, in consideration of the ongoing business advisory, fund raising and readiness services rendered under this Mandate.

Success Fee

In consideration of the fund raising services rendered, {CLIENT_SHORT} will pay Sample Advisory a success fee equivalent to certain percentage of the total funds raised through any transaction sourced, facilitated and substantially assisted by Sample Advisory, of which the percentage of Success Fees shall be decided mutually as and when applicable. The success fee becomes payable upon successful completion of the relevant transaction and receipt of the corresponding funds by {CLIENT_SHORT}, within five business days of such receipt. Where a transaction comprises multiple tranches, the success fee will be computed and become payable in respect of each tranche as funds are received.

Statutory Costs and Expenses

All statutory costs, regulatory charges, legal expenses, due diligence expenses, valuation expenses and other transaction related expenses will be borne directly by {CLIENT_SHORT}, separately from the fees set out above. Reasonable out of pocket expenses incurred by Sample Advisory in performing the services, including travel and documentation, will be reimbursed by {CLIENT_SHORT} on an actual basis.

This fee structure may be revised by mutual agreement between the parties, recorded in writing, as the scope of the engagement evolves.

12. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Mandate is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising under this Mandate will first be addressed through good faith discussion between the parties, and, failing an amicable resolution, will be referred to and finally resolved through arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, by a sole arbitrator mutually appointed by the parties. The seat and venue of arbitration will be Mumbai, Maharashtra, with proceedings conducted in English.
