# Engagement letter — clause on technology and AI (draft)

*Draft prepared for the firm's review. It is not legal advice. Please have it vetted by your legal counsel before use, and keep it consistent with your privacy notice under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.*

---

**Use of technology and artificial intelligence**

1. **Recording system.** We use *Vouchr*, an expense-recording application, to capture, classify and review your purchase bills, payments and related documents. Original documents are kept in a Google Drive folder maintained by us for you, organised by financial year.

2. **AI assistance.** To support our team, Vouchr may send copies of specific documents and data to the Google Gemini API, provided by Google, under Google's paid-service terms. Examples are a bill image, a bank-statement narration or a tax notice. AI is used to read documents, suggest classifications and flag possible errors or irregularities. Under those paid-service terms, Google states that it does not use such content to improve its products. Such processing may take place outside India.

3. **AI output is never final.** Every AI output is a suggestion. It is checked automatically against fixed rules: formats, GSTIN check digits, arithmetic, and our own approved masters. Any value the system is not certain of is marked for human review. No entry is finalised, no tax is computed and no return is filed on the basis of AI output alone. Responsibility for our professional work remains entirely with us.

4. **Minimum data.** We send only what a task needs. We do not use your data to train any AI model.

5. **Your choice.** You may ask in writing that your documents are not processed with AI. We will then carry out the work without these features. This may affect timelines.

6. **Personal data.** Your records may contain personal data, such as the names and PANs of individual suppliers. You confirm that you are entitled to share such data with us for these purposes. We will process it in accordance with applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and our privacy notice.

---

**हिंदी सार (ग्राहक के लिए):** हम आपके बिल और भुगतान *Vouchr* ऐप में दर्ज करते हैं। मूल दस्तावेज़ Google Drive में रखे जाते हैं। ज़रूरत होने पर कुछ दस्तावेज़ Google Gemini AI (पेड सेवा) को पढ़ने और जाँच के सुझाव के लिए भेजे जा सकते हैं। Google के अनुसार इस सेवा में आपकी जानकारी उसके उत्पाद सुधारने में इस्तेमाल नहीं होती। यह प्रोसेसिंग भारत के बाहर भी हो सकती है। AI का हर सुझाव नियमों से जाँचा जाता है और हमारी टीम उसे देखती है। जिस बात पर ज़रा भी शक हो, उसे अलग से चिह्नित किया जाता है। केवल AI के भरोसे कोई एंट्री या रिटर्न अंतिम नहीं होता। आप लिखित में AI के इस्तेमाल से मना कर सकते हैं।
