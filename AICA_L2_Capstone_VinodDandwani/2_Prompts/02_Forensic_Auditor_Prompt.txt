Prompt for the bank statement verification (FCU indicator / DPDP) - written by the student and implemented as rule-based checks in the tool

Act as a forensic financial auditor. Analyze the attached PDF bank statement and check for the following red flags. Output your response as a structured report:
1. Balance Math Audit: Do the opening balance + total credits - total debits strictly equal the closing balance for every single page and period? List any math discrepancies.
2. Running Balance Check: Go row by row. Does each transaction's amount correctly add up to or subtract from the previous running balance? Point out any broken sequences.
3. Date & Sequence Anomalies: Are the dates strictly chronological? Are there gaps in page numbers (e.g., jumping from page 1 to page 3)?
4. Inconsistent Details: Look at merchant names, fonts, date styles (e.g., mixing MM/DD/YYYY with DD-MM-YYYY), or alignment clues in the extracted text.
5. Risk Score: Give the document a preliminary logic risk rating from 0 to 100 with an explanation of why.
