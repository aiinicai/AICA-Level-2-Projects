AUDIT OBSERVATIONS AND RECTIFICATION REQUIRED - Sunrise Heights Co-operative Housing Society Ltd., FY 2025-26

OBS-01 Dishonoured cheque (Bank) - Severity: High
Observation: Cheque received from flat B-301 (Rs 5,159, receipt R-0233 dated 17-Jul-2025) was returned unpaid by the bank, but the receipt stands in the member's ledger and the member shows no arrears for that month.
Provision: Bye-law 70; Negotiable Instruments Act s.138
Financial effect: Rs 5,159
Rectification required: Reverse the receipt, debit cheque return charges to the member, issue a s.138 notice if not regularised within 15 days, and add a monthly bank reconciliation review to the Treasurer's checklist.

OBS-02 Statutory calendar (Compliance) - Severity: High
Observation: As at 15-Jul-2026, 5 statutory obligations are overdue: Audit rectification report (Form O) for FY 2024-25; Section 101 recovery applications against chronic defaulters; Structural audit of building; Transfer sinking fund collections to earmarked FD; Deemed conveyance application. Falling due within 45 days: TDS return Form 26Q - Q1 FY 2026-27; Fire safety certificate Form B (half-yearly); Statutory audit of FY 2025-26 accounts
Provision: MCS Act 1960 ss.75, 79, 81, 82, 101; bye-laws 15, 77, 97; Maharashtra Lifts Act 2017; Income-tax Act s.139(1)
Rectification required: Adopt the compliance calendar as a standing agenda item in every committee meeting; assign an owner to each obligation; the Secretary to report status monthly.

OBS-03 Duplicate payment (Expenses) - Severity: High
Observation: Housekeeping invoice SRV/2025/08-14 of Rs 52,650 was paid twice (PV in Aug-2025 and again 19 days later). No invoice-number uniqueness control exists in the payment process.
Provision: Bye-law 142-143; internal control
Financial effect: Rs 52,650
Rectification required: Recover the excess from Swachh Housekeeping Services against the next bill; enforce invoice-number uniqueness in the accounting software.

OBS-04 Split invoices / tendering (Expenses) - Severity: High
Observation: Terrace waterproofing of Rs 1,45,000 was awarded on a single quotation and billed as three invoices of Rs 49,000, 48,000 and 48,000 on consecutive days, each below the Rs 50,000 committee limit fixed by AGM resolution A.4.
Provision: AGM 2025 resolution A.4; bye-law 143; MCS Act s.73 (duties of committee)
Financial effect: Rs 1,45,000
Rectification required: Obtain ex-post ratification from the General Body; adopt a written procurement policy (three quotations above Rs 50,000, tender above Rs 5 lakh).

OBS-05 Related-party vendor (Expenses) - Severity: High
Observation: Patil Plumbing Works, paid Rs 1,21,550 during the year across six vouchers, has its address at flat B-203, the Treasurer's residence. All vouchers were approved by the Treasurer alone and the interest was not disclosed in any minutes.
Provision: MCS Act s.73(1AB)/s.78 (conflict of interest, removal of office bearer); bye-law 143
Financial effect: Rs 1,21,550
Rectification required: Disclose the interest to the committee and General Body; the Treasurer should abstain from approving these payments; obtain independent quotes for plumbing work.

OBS-06 Sinking fund diversion (Funds) - Severity: High
Observation: Sinking fund contributions of Rs 5,32,001 were collected but only Rs 4,80,000 was credited to the Sinking Fund; Rs 52,001 was used for revenue expenditure (MC minutes 15-Feb-2026 acknowledge temporary use).
Provision: Bye-law 13(a) and 15; MCS Act s.70 (investment of funds)
Financial effect: Rs 52,001
Rectification required: Restore the diverted amount to the sinking fund immediately and invest it; sinking fund may be used only for structural repairs with General Body approval.

OBS-07 Sinking fund investment (Funds) - Severity: High
Observation: Sinking Fund balance of Rs 43,82,001 is backed by earmarked fixed deposits of only Rs 32,00,000, an under-investment of Rs 11,82,001.
Provision: Bye-law 15 read with MCS Act s.70 and Rule 54 (investment in co-operative bank/approved securities)
Financial effect: Rs 11,82,001
Rectification required: Invest the shortfall in fixed deposits with a co-operative bank earmarked to the Sinking Fund and report the position to the AGM.

OBS-08 Maintenance bills (GST) - Severity: High
Observation: Aggregate collections of Rs 62,44,481 exceed the Rs 20 lakh registration threshold, so GST applies to members whose monthly contribution exceeds Rs 7,500 (whole amount taxable, Circular 109/28/2019). Testing all 768 bills: GST over-charged by Rs 82,182 on 201 bills (17 flats) and under-charged by Rs 4,302 on 3 bills. Causes: (i) property tax included in the taxable value for 3BHK flats; (ii) flat A-201 charged GST though below the limit; (iii) flat B-604 not charged for Jun-Aug 2025.
Provision: CGST Act s.22, s.15; Notification 12/2017-CT(R) entry 77(c); Circular 109/28/2019-GST dated 22-Jul-2019
Financial effect: Rs 86,483
Rectification required: Issue credit notes u/s 34 for the over-charge and debit notes for the under-charge within the time limit; correct the billing template to exclude property tax and apply the Rs 7,500 test per member per month; reconcile GSTR-1 with the corrected bills.

OBS-09 Meetings - notice and quorum (Governance) - Severity: High
Observation: MC meeting of 08-Jun-2025: Notice period 6 days < 7 required; AGM meeting of 28-Sep-2025: Notice period 9 days < 14 required; MC meeting of 12-Oct-2025: Notice period 2 days < 7 required; No quorum (4 of 11, need 6) - expenditure approved. Resolutions passed at the 12-Oct-2025 meeting (cash payment to painter, Diwali bonus) are liable to be treated as invalid.
Provision: Bye-laws 97 (AGM notice 14 clear days), 100 (AGM quorum), 128-131 (committee meetings and quorum); MCS Act s.75
Rectification required: Ratify the affected resolutions at a properly convened meeting; the Secretary to maintain a notice register with proof of dispatch; record quorum at the start of every meeting.

OBS-10 Follow-up of committee decisions (Governance) - Severity: High
Observation: Of 18 action items recorded in minutes, 7 remain not complied as at 15-Jul-2026: Secretary to obtain three quotations for terrace waterproofing (due 01-Jun-2025); Secretary to file audit rectification report (Form O) for FY 2024-25 (due 22-Nov-2025); Committee to appoint structural engineer (due 31-Jan-2026); Secretary to file section 101 recovery applications (due 31-Dec-2025); Treasurer to reconcile bank statement for Nov-2025 (due 10-Dec-2025); Secretary to obtain quotations from empanelled structural engineers (due 31-Jan-2026); Treasurer to transfer sinking fund collections to the earmarked FD (due 31-Mar-2026)
Provision: MCS Act s.73 (duties of committee); bye-laws 139-140 (functions of Secretary and Treasurer)
Rectification required: Maintain an action tracker reviewed at the start of each meeting; link each item to an owner and a due date; report open items to the AGM.

OBS-11 Arrears and defaulters (Member Ledger) - Severity: High
Observation: Closing member dues at 31-Mar-2026 are Rs 10,37,561 against annual billing of Rs 67,23,780 (15.4%). 10 members are in arrears of more than 3 months, of which 8 exceed 6 months. Largest: A-303 Rs 2,67,247, B-703 Rs 2,02,966, A-103 Rs 1,59,681
Provision: MCS Act 1960 s.101 (recovery certificate); bye-laws 70-72 (dues and interest)
Financial effect: Rs 10,01,324
Rectification required: Serve final demand notices and file s.101 recovery applications with the Deputy Registrar for all members above 3 months; disclose defaulters in the AGM agenda as required.

OBS-12 Interest on arrears (Member Ledger) - Severity: High
Observation: Interest on arrears has been short-charged by Rs 1,580 in 3 member accounts when recomputed at the 21% p.a. adopted by the General Body (MC resolution 1.2 of 13-Apr-2025). Office-bearer flat(s) A-402 were charged no interest at all despite paying 20-45 days late. Flats B-101, B-502 were charged at 18% instead of 21%.
Provision: Bye-law 72 (simple interest not exceeding 21% p.a.); MCS Act s.78 (misconduct of office bearers)
Financial effect: Rs 1,580
Rectification required: Raise supplementary debit notes for the short interest; apply the rate uniformly through the billing software with no manual override; the committee should record a resolution on the waiver to the Secretary's account and the member should make good the amount.

OBS-13 Short/non-deduction of TDS (Tax - TDS) - Severity: High
Observation: TDS short-deducted by Rs 24,016: no TDS on accounting fees of Rs 96,000 to R. K. Kadam & Associates (s.194J, threshold Rs 50,000); security agency SecureGuard (a partnership firm) deducted at 1% instead of 2% u/s 194C on Rs 13.2 lakh; cash vendors without PAN attract 20% u/s 206AA. Estimated interest u/s 201(1A) about Rs 1,441.
Provision: Income-tax Act s.194C, 194J, 206AA, 201(1A); s.40(a)(ia)
Financial effect: Rs 25,456
Rectification required: Deposit the short TDS with interest, file correction statements (Form 26Q), and maintain a vendor master with PAN and entity type driving the TDS rate automatically.

OBS-14 Unidentified credits (Bank) - Severity: Medium
Observation: 1 credit(s) totalling Rs 6,770 appear in the bank statement with no corresponding receipt (e.g. NEFT from 'RAVI' on 12-Nov-2025). A member may be wrongly shown in arrears.
Provision: Bye-law 142 (books of account); AS/Accounting discipline
Financial effect: Rs 6,770
Rectification required: Identify the payer, issue the receipt and credit the member's ledger; keep a suspense account for unidentified credits reviewed monthly.

OBS-15 Monthly reconciliation (Bank) - Severity: Medium
Observation: No bank reconciliation statement was prepared during the year. A reconstructed reconciliation explains the difference between books and bank through 1 dishonoured cheque, 1 unidentified credit and unbooked bank charges of Rs 3,127, leaving an unreconciled balance of Rs 0.
Provision: Bye-law 142; MCS Rules 65 (books of account)
Rectification required: Prepare and sign a bank reconciliation every month; book bank charges from the statement.

OBS-16 Cash payments (Expenses) - Severity: Medium
Observation: 2 cash payments above Rs 10,000 totalling Rs 42,500 (Sai Painter (cash), Vijay Electricals (cash)), approved by a single office bearer and without vendor PAN.
Provision: Bye-law 143; Income-tax Act s.40A(3) discipline; MCS Rules on cash limit
Financial effect: Rs 42,500
Rectification required: Pay vendors only through banking channels; obtain PAN/declaration for every vendor above Rs 10,000.

OBS-17 Reserve fund transfer (Funds) - Severity: Medium
Observation: Only Rs 50,000 transferred to Reserve Fund against the minimum 25% of net profit of FY 2024-25 (Rs 1,03,000 on Rs 4,12,000).
Provision: MCS Act 1960 s.66(1); bye-law 14
Financial effect: Rs 53,000
Rectification required: Transfer the balance of Rs 53,000 before finalising accounts.

OBS-18 Resolutions vs records (Governance) - Severity: Medium
Observation: MC resolution 3.1 (08-Jun-2025) awarded Rs 1,45,000 waterproofing on one quotation, contrary to AGM resolution A.4 (three quotations above Rs 50,000). Resolution 11.2 (15-Feb-2026) records that sinking fund collections were used for revenue expenses, which bye-law 15 prohibits. Resolution 7.1 (12-Oct-2025) approved a cash payment of Rs 28,000, above the cash limit.
Provision: AGM 2025 resolution A.4; bye-laws 15, 143
Rectification required: Place these matters before the General Body for ratification and record corrective resolutions.

OBS-19 Advance tax and interest (Tax - Income tax) - Severity: Medium
Observation: Tax liability after TDS is Rs 61,504 (normal provisions with s.80P(2)(d) deduction of Rs 2,45,000 is more beneficial than s.115BAD by Rs 43,549). No advance tax was paid although liability exceeds Rs 10,000, attracting interest of about Rs 7,411 u/s 234B/234C. Return in ITR-5 is due 31-Oct-2026.
Provision: Income-tax Act ss.80P(2)(d), 115BAD, 208, 234B, 234C, 139(1); CIT v. Totgars (SC) and Mavilayi (SC) on 80P
Financial effect: Rs 68,915
Rectification required: Pay the self-assessment tax with interest before filing; set a calendar for advance tax instalments (15 Jun/Sep/Dec/Mar) based on tower rent and bank interest.

OBS-20 Benford first-digit test (Expenses) - Severity: Low
Observation: First-digit distribution of 105 expense invoices deviates from Benford's expectation by at most 14.9 percentage points (digit 1); the recurring fixed contracts explain most of the skew.
Provision: ISA/SA 520 analytical procedures
Rectification required: No action beyond the specific anomalies already reported.

OBS-21 Interest on delayed payment (GST) - Severity: Low
Observation: Interest on arrears of Rs 1,33,722 billed to members has not been included in the value of supply for members who are liable to GST.
Provision: CGST Act s.15(2)(d)
Financial effect: Rs 2,124
Rectification required: Include interest in the taxable value for GST-liable members, or obtain a written position from the GST consultant.

OBS-22 Mutuality and 80P position (Tax - Income tax) - Severity: Low
Observation: Receipts from members of Rs 63,78,203 and transfer premium of Rs 75,000 are treated as mutual. Transfer premium is within the Rs 25,000 cap; interest from the co-operative bank (Rs 2,45,000) is claimed u/s 80P(2)(d). Interest from HDFC Bank and tower rent are taxable.
Provision: Bombay HC in Sind CHS (2009) and Mittal Court Premises CHS; s.80P(2)(d); Totgars (SC 2010) distinguished
Rectification required: Document the mutuality position in the tax file; obtain Form 26AS and reconcile TDS of Rs 45,200.

Overall: 22 observations were raised of which 13 are high severity. The pattern of findings (interest waiver, related-party payments, split invoices, fund diversion and missing reconciliations) indicates that the committee has not operated basic financial controls during the year. The society is advised to adopt the recommendations above and report compliance in the audit rectification report (Form O).
