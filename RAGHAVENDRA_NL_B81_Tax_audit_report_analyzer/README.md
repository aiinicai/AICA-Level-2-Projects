Problem Statement
Form No. 3CD, prescribed under Rule 6G(2) of the Income-tax Rules, 1962, requires a tax auditor to report over 44 clauses of granular particulars — depreciation, TDS on provision-related disallowances under section 40(a), MSME payment delays, section 43B reporting, gratuity provisions, related-party payments under section 40A(2)(b), and more — each governed by its own statutory computation logic. Preparing an audit-ready summary from this form, and translating it into a draft computation of total income, is today a manual, repetitive, and error-prone exercise: the auditor must trace each figure back to its exact clause, apply the correct statutory disallowance percentage, and re-key everything into a separate computation workbook, often taking upward of two hours per audit with no structured, reviewable output. Despite the form being available as a structured JSON export from the income-tax e-filing utility, no existing tool converts this data directly into a clause-referenced, audit-ready review pack — while any such automation must still operate strictly as a decision-support aid, preserving the auditor's professional judgment and independent verification obligations under the Standards on Auditing and the ICAI Guidance Note on Tax Audit. This project addresses that gap: an AI-assisted tool that parses the Form 3CD e-filing JSON and produces a clause-referenced financial summary, a full 44-clause dashboard, and a draft tax computation — in Excel, HTML, PDF and Word — complete with a built-in Tax Head sign-off workflow, reducing a ~2-hour manual review to roughly 5 to 10 minutes while remaining explicit that it supplements, never replaces, the auditor's own verification.
