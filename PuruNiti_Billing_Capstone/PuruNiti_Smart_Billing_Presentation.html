1. The Problem Statement

Why did we build the PuruNiti Smart Billing Suite?

📝

Manual Word/Excel Invoicing

Small taxpayers and professionals (lawyers, doctors, consultants) frequently do not use tailored software and prepare invoices in Word or Excel due to unfriendly interfaces or complex configurations.

📊

Lagging Behind on Analytics

Relying on separate manual document registers makes it impossible to gather system-level financial analytics, revenue trends, outstanding dues, or accurate tax breakdowns.

⚖️

compliance & XML Sync Errors

General templates lack validations, showing Cess on service items or breaking RCM outward supplies. Additionally, syncing with Tally Prime registers requires manual double-entry.

✨

The PuruNiti Opportunity

A wonderful opportunity to streamline billing workflows with an extremely friendly, easy-to-use user interface that links CRM directories, timesheet calculations, and Tally Prime exports seamlessly.

User Authentication & Role Security

Ensuring local database protection and staff privilege segregation:

🔒 Local Security & Recovery

  • 🔑 Salted SHA256 Hashing: Passwords and security answers are protected using unique random salts generated per user profile.
  • 🔄 Self-Service Recovery: Verification of security questions enables offline password resets directly from the login window.
  • 👥 Multi-Account Management: Admin panel allows provisioning, viewing, and deleting user access logs.

👥 Role-Based Permissions (RBAC)

👑 Administrator

Full access. Can delete invoices, log/delete payments, modify DB configuration settings, view analytics dashboard, and manage user accounts.

🛠️ Staff / Operator

Restricted access. Permitted to create new invoices, view registers, and log collections. Blocked from editing saved invoices, deleting receipts, and viewing dashboards.

Outstanding Payments & Aging Analytics

Tracking invoice settlements and managing aging accounts receivable:

💸 Receipts Ledger Pipeline

  • ➕ Payment Logging: Log payment receipts with date, payment mode (UPI, Bank, Cash, Cheque), reference UTR, and comments.
  • 📊 Period-Based Ledger: View and audit receipts received during a specific Month, Last Month, Financial Year, or Custom range.
  • 🔄 Outstanding Math: Automatically computes unpaid balances by subtracting payments from grand invoice totals.

📆 Account Aging Analysis

  • 🗂️ Dynamic Aging Columns: Sorts outstanding customer balances across user-defined aging brackets (e.g. 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days).
  • 📈 Actionable Insights: Identify lagging customer accounts and track collection delays in real-time.
  • 📥 Excel Reports: One-click export for Outstanding Lists, Aging analysis grids, and Receipts Ledgers for audit sync.
