# Capstone Video Checklist (ICAI requirement)

ICAI (AICA Level 2, Annexure D) requires a video **showing both your face and the technical content**. Upload it to **YouTube (Unlisted)** or **Google Drive**, set to "anyone with the link can view", and submit the link in the **same Google Form** as the ZIP. The video is **not** put inside the ZIP.

## Suggested recording setup
- Use Zoom, Google Meet (record yourself presenting), or Microsoft Teams, with **camera on** and **screen shared**.
- Keep the face bubble in a corner while the app is on screen.
- Length: 5–8 minutes. Follow `06_Presentation_Script_5_Minutes.md`.

## Demo sequence (about 3 minutes, in Demo Mode)
1. Double-click `start_local.bat`. The browser opens the Dashboard.
2. **Sales → Download filled sample → Upload & Validate.** Show "0 errors" and the Dr/Cr preview.
3. **Confirm & Post.** Show "Status: SUCCESS" and the per-voucher result.
4. Upload a file with a mistake (for example, change a debit so the journal doesn't balance, or misspell a ledger). Show the row/column error messages and that posting is disabled.
5. **Create Ledgers:** show the missing-ledger suggestions (optional).
6. **Import History** and **Audit Log.**
7. **Tally → Connection** (with real TallyPrime open, if available).

## Before uploading
- [ ] Face visible throughout
- [ ] Audio clear
- [ ] Link set to "Anyone with the link"
- [ ] ZIP under 100 MB, submitted within 10 days of batch completion
