Summary

Tata Steel makes flat and long steel products in India and overseas. Q1FY27 revenue rose 14.3% YoY to Rs 60,794 crore (NSE results filing) and profit excluding one-offs rose 17.5% YoY. Provisional Q2FY27 data shows India crude steel production up about 10% YoY to 6.21 million tonnes and sales up 7%. The board meeting for Q2FY27 results was moved from 27 October to 7 November 2026, and the NSE filings confirm that no newer results were published as of 1 November. The model target of Rs 217.1 implies 29.5% upside and is in line with the consensus mean of Rs 217.0; the composite score of 50 sits exactly at the quality gate, and conviction is Low because the DCF value of Rs 51.17 points the other way. Analyst view: the BUY rests on an earnings recovery that consensus prices at 1.9x trailing EPS; with net debt to equity of 0.82x and the price 13.5% below its 200-day average, we would treat it as a low-conviction call until Q2FY27 shows the margin gain in reported profit.Analyst view

Company profile

Tata Steel Limited engages in the manufacture and distribution of steel products in India and internationally. It offers reinforcement bars (rebars) and wire rods, cut-and-bend reinforcement bars, welded wire mesh, prefabricated cages (pre-cages), steel couplers and carpet reinforcement; hot-rolled, cold rolled, coated coil, tubes, rebar, wire rods; and metallic coated, pre-finished steels, alloy steels, and profiles and construction systems. The company also provides solutions in building envelopes, structural, fit-out, foundations, and highway engineering products, as well as operates steel service centers. It serves agricultural, automotive steels, construction, consumer goods, energy and power, engineering, and material handling industries. Methodology and rating key

▲ BUY: upside 15% or more◆ HOLD: -5% to 15%▼ SELL: below -5%Provisional: never BUY
  • Earnings basis. EBITDA excludes other income and unusual items. Net profit is profit attributable to owners. One-off items are the source's unusual items plus any spike in other income (more than three times the median of the other periods, with the excess at least 10% of profit before tax); they are removed after tax at the period's effective rate for EPS, P/E history, growth scores and the target. This is an estimate; company-defined adjusted figures can differ. Quarterly comparisons are matched by period end date. Trailing EPS uses four consecutive quarters, or the latest full year when a quarter is missing.
  • Target price. Weighted blend of a relative multiple (40%), an intrinsic method (30%: free cash flow DCF for non-financials, justified P/B for lenders) and the street consensus mean (30%). A method more than 2.5x or less than 0.4x the current price is excluded as an outlier; weights are renormalised. The blended target is capped at 1.15x the highest analyst target.
  • Relative multiple. Forward EPS (consensus where it is between 0.5x and 1.4x of trailing EPS; capped at 1.4x trailing when consensus expects a sharper rebound, because a historical P/E applied to recovery earnings double counts the recovery; otherwise trailing EPS grown at the 3-year EPS CAGR bounded to -10% and 20%) times the median of the company's FY-end P/E history and the peer median P/E, bounded to 5x and 80x and capped at 1.25x the current P/E, so that no more than a 25% re-rating is assumed within 12 months. Lenders use forward book value and P/B with the same cap. Scenarios: bear case earnings 15% lower at 0.8x the lower of current and target multiple; bull case earnings 10% higher at 1.15x the target multiple.
  • DCF. Base free cash flow is the average of the last three years. Growth for years 1 to 5 equals the revenue CAGR bounded to 3% and 15%, fading to terminal growth by year 10. The present value is rolled forward 12 months at the cost of equity less the dividend yield. An excluded method that lies on the other side of the current price from the target lowers conviction to Low.
  • Gates. A BUY with composite score under 50 is cut to HOLD. A HOLD with score under 35 and upside under 5% becomes SELL. The rating is provisional, cannot be BUY and has Low conviction when only one valuation method is usable, a download the rating depends on failed, quarterly results are past their SEBI LODR deadline (45 days; 60 for the March quarter) but missing from the data, or research found newer results or a data conflict. Otherwise conviction reflects the score and the spread between methods.
  • Scorecard thresholds (0 to 100). ROE 5% to 22% (lenders 8% to 18%); ROCE 6% to 25%; net debt to equity 1.5x to 0x; interest cover 1.5x to 10x; CFO to PAT 0.5x to 1.1x; revenue CAGR 0% to 15%; EPS CAGR -5% to 20%; P/E vs own median 1.4x to 0.8x; PEG on forward growth 3 to 1; free cash flow yield 0% to 5%; price vs 200-DMA -15% to +10%; relative return vs Nifty -15% to +15%; volatility 50% to 18%; drawdown -50% to -12%; beta 1.6 to 0.7. Upside to the model's own target is not scored.
  • Data. Prices, annual statements, consensus and peer data from Yahoo Finance via the open-source yfinance library, fetched 01-Nov-2026 18:51 IST. Quarterly results, results board meeting dates and the shareholding pattern come from NSE filings (XBRL) where available; a filed quarter replaces Yahoo's figure for the same quarter, and differences above 2% (revenue) or 5% (profit) are listed in the data notes. If the exchange cannot be reached, the report uses Yahoo alone and says so. Figures are consolidated where the company reports them. Rs crore = Rs 10 million.
  • Freshness. Results filed with the exchange, or considered at a results board meeting, for a quarter that is not in the model data make the rating provisional. A board meeting followed by a later meeting for the same quarter is treated as rescheduled.
