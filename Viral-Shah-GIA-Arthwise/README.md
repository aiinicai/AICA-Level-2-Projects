## 1. Executive Summary & Capabilities

The **GIA_Arthwise Loan Facilitator Platform** is a production-oriented, full-stack credit proposal evaluation and debt syndication suite engineered specifically for the Indian lending ecosystem. It supports Indian number formatting (Rupees, Lakhs, Crores), April–March fiscal years, and RBI credit governance guidelines.

### Core Capabilities
1. **Multi-Constitution Borrower Support**: Salaried individuals, Self-Employed Professionals, Proprietorships, Partnerships, LLPs, Private Limited Companies, and Public Limited Companies.
2. **Comprehensive Loan Products**:
   - Home Loans (Purchase & Construction)
   - Loan Against Property (LAP)
   - Secured & Unsecured Business Loans
   - Cash Credit & Overdraft (CC/OD)
   - Working Capital Facilities
   - Project Finance & Term Loans
   - Balance Transfers & Top-up Loans
3. **Primary Document Checklist & Verification Governance**:
   - Standard 7-column checklist: `Document | Applicable? | Received? | Period Required | Verification Status | Missing Details | Remarks`.
   - Detailed status flow: `requested`, `partially_received`, `received`, `verified`, `rejected`, `expired`, `superseded`, `not_applicable`.
   - **Enforced Governance**: "Received" does not imply "verified". Marking any standard requirement as "Not Applicable" strictly mandates an analyst justification reason.
4. **Local-First Extraction & Side-by-Side Review**:
   - Ingests PDFs, scanned documents, Excel workbooks (`.xlsx`), and CSVs.
   - Extracts PAN, GSTINs, ITR-V data, GSTR-3B turnovers, bank credits/debits, EMI sheets, and customer purchase orders.
   - Preserves source file, page/sheet, raw string, normalized `Decimal` amount, extraction confidence, and audit verification history.
5. **Deterministic Financial Underwriting Engine**:
   - Strict `decimal.Decimal` arithmetic with `ROUND_HALF_UP` precision.
   - Calculates EMI, Amortization schedules, FOIR, Collateral LTV, DSCR (standard & gross cash accrual), ICR, Current & Quick ratios, Debt-Equity, TOL/TNW, and Net Cash Conversion Cycle (Debtor, Creditor, Inventory days).
   - Identifies the exact **Binding Limiting Constraint** (FOIR vs LTV vs DSCR vs Product Cap).
6. **9-Year Credit Monitoring Arrangement (CMA) Workspace**:
   - 3 Historical Audited Years + 1 Provisional Year + 5 Projected Years.
   - Line-item schedules for Form II (Operating Statement), Form III (Balance Sheet), Form V (Working Capital: Nayak Committee Turnover Method & Tandon Committee Method II MPBF), and Form VII (Key Ratios).
   - Real-time mathematical balancing check (verifies Assets == Liabilities across all periods with zero unexplained balancing plugs).
7. **Lender Policy Library & Policy Match Matrix**:
   - Administrator-managed policies for State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Bank of Baroda, and Bajaj Housing/Finserve.
   - Automatically computes policy fit (`ELIGIBLE`, `BORDERLINE`, `INELIGIBLE`), deviation triggers, and missing documentation.
8. **Multi-Format Institutional Report Exporter**:
   - **Excel (.xlsx)**: 9-Year CMA statement with formatted tables and formula protection.
   - **Word (.docx)**: Detailed Project Report (DPR) / Bank TEV dossier with executive summary and mitigants.
   - **PDF (.pdf)**: Executive Credit Appraisal Memorandum with header, table of limits, preliminary notice, and signoff block.
9. **Role-Based Access Control & Privacy**:
   - Roles: `admin`, `analyst`, `reviewer`, and `client`.
   - Client isolation prevents borrowers from seeing other proposals.
   - PAN, Aadhaar, and Bank Account masking by default.
   - Spreadsheet formula injection protection (neutralizes `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`).
   - Immutable audit logging for all creation, updates, views, and downloads.

## 4. Financial Calculation Definitions & Formulas

All calculations use Python `decimal.Decimal` with explicit `ROUND_HALF_UP` to prevent floating-point drift:

### 1. Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)
$$\text{EMI} = P \times r \times \frac{(1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}$$
- $P$ = Principal loan amount
- $r$ = Monthly interest rate ($\text{Annual Rate} / 1200$)
- $n$ = Tenure in months
- *Edge case*: If $r = 0$, $\text{EMI} = P / n$.

### 2. Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)
$$\text{FOIR} = \frac{\sum \text{Existing Monthly EMIs} + \text{Proposed EMI}}{\text{Net Monthly Income / Monthly Cash Accruals}} \times 100$$
$$\text{Max Proposed EMI} = \max(0, (\text{Monthly Income} \times \text{Max FOIR Policy}) - \sum \text{Existing EMIs})$$
$$\text{FOIR-Based Eligible Loan} = \text{Max Proposed EMI} \times \frac{(1 + r)^n - 1}{r(1 + r)^n}$$

### 3. Loan to Value (LTV)
$$\text{LTV} = \frac{\text{Proposed Loan Amount}}{\text{Assessed Collateral Market Value}} \times 100$$
$$\text{LTV-Based Eligible Loan} = \text{Collateral Market Value} \times \text{Max LTV Policy}$$

### 4. Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
$$\text{DSCR} = \frac{\text{PAT} + \text{Depreciation} + \text{Finance Costs}}{\text{Annual Principal Repayments} + \text{Finance Costs}}$$
- Benchmark: Standard bank requirement is minimum $1.35\times$ to $1.50\times$.

### 5. Working Capital Assessment Methods
1. **Nayak Committee (Turnover Method - SME Limits up to ₹5 Crores)**:
   - Total Working Capital Requirement = $25\% \times \text{Turnover}$
   - Minimum Promoter Margin = $5\% \times \text{Turnover}$
   - Assessed Bank Finance Limit (MPBF) = $20\% \times \text{Turnover}$
2. **Tandon Committee Method II (MPBF)**:
   - Working Capital Gap (WCG) = $\text{Total Current Assets} - \text{Non-Bank Current Liabilities}$
   - Minimum Net Working Capital (Margin) = $25\% \times \text{Total Current Assets}$
   - Maximum Permissible Bank Finance = $\max(0, \text{WCG} - \text{Margin})$

### 6. Binding Limiting Factor Resolution
$$\text{Preliminary Eligible Loan} = \min(L_{\text{FOIR}}, L_{\text{LTV}}, L_{\text{DSCR}}, L_{\text{ProductMax}}, \text{Requested Amount})$$
The system explicitly identifies which parameter is the **binding limiting constraint** and presents a step-by-step breakdown explaining why the amount is capped, or if any critical input is missing.

## 6. Docker & Cloud VPS Deployment Guide

### Deployment with Docker Compose
```bash
# 1. Clone repository on VPS
git clone <repo_url>
cd "GIA_Arthwise"

# 2. Configure production environment
cp .env.example .env
# Edit .env and supply a strong SECRET_KEY

# 3. Launch full stack (PostgreSQL + FastAPI + Nginx PWA)
docker compose up -d --build

# 4. Verify status
docker compose ps
```

### Shared Hosting (cPanel / Apache / PHP) Assessment
> [!WARNING]
> **Technical Assessment on Shared Hosting**:
> Generic shared cPanel hosting is **insufficient and unsupported** for this application:
> 1. Shared hosting environments typically run PHP/Apache and do not support long-running Python ASGI daemons (`uvicorn`).
> 2. Document extraction engines require native shared C libraries (`poppler`, `mupdf`, `libxml2`) and process memory ceilings that exceed shared cPanel execution limits (typically 128 MB or 30-second execution timeouts).
> 3. Background extraction and ReportLab document rendering will trigger resource killing by CloudLinux LVE.
>
> **Recommended Hosting Infrastructure**:
> - Cloud VPS / VM (DigitalOcean Droplet, AWS EC2, GCP Compute Engine, or Hetzner VPS).
> - Minimum Specs: 2 vCPUs, 4 GB RAM, 40 GB SSD.
> - OS: Ubuntu 22.04 LTS / Debian 12 with Docker.

## 7. Security, Privacy & DPDP Compliance

- **Deny-by-Default Role Authorization**: Enforced on every single API endpoint.
- **Client Isolation**: Client user accounts are mathematically constrained to records matching their assigned `client_id`.
- **Sensitive Identifier Masking**:
  - PAN: `AC*****P` (e.g. `ACAPX0000P`)
  - GSTIN: `24********1Z5`
  - Bank Account: `**********5678`
- **Spreadsheet Formula Injection Prevention**: Any untrusted user input exported to Excel beginning with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, `\t`, or `\r` is automatically sanitized by prefixing with a single quote (`'`).
- **Private Storage & Anti-Traversal**: Uploaded documents are saved under random UUID filenames with sanitized extensions. Path traversal attempts are blocked.
- **External API Adapter Policy**: Third-party API integrations (Protean PAN, GST Portal, CIBIL Bureau, Account Aggregator) are explicitly configured in sandbox mode and disabled by default until signed borrower consent declarations are executed.

## 10. Known Limitations & Remaining Operational Steps

1. **Third-Party Live Integrations**: Active API credentials for Protean GSP (PAN/GSTIN), TransUnion CIBIL, and Account Aggregator require bilateral commercial agreements with respective providers and are kept disabled in sandbox mode.
2. **Scanned PDF OCR**: Local PDF extraction parses digital PDFs and vector text natively. Poor-quality camera scans require local Tesseract installation (`brew install tesseract` / `apt-get install tesseract-ocr`) or opt-in cloud OCR with signed DPDP consent.
3. **Legal Vetting**: Title search reports, non-encumbrance certificates, and property valuation reports must be validated by panel advocates and approved valuers prior to bank submission.
