SME GSTAT AppealKit - Professional v0.4.0
Release date: 06 August 2026

FIXED
- Removed the five-line cut that truncated long cover subjects.
- Removed the two-line cut that hid the end of master-index particulars.
- Changed index rows to variable height so the full particulars remain visible.
- Corrected variable-height row geometry so highlighted rows cannot cover column headings.
- Separated case title, volume indicator and index-page indicator to prevent overwriting.
- Prevented duplicate wording such as "ANNEXURE Annexure A-1".

ADDED
- Editable/optional cover heading and organisation line.
- Optional, custom or blank document/appeal type.
- Optional case title on master-index pages.
- Numeric, alphabetic and Roman annexure numbering.
- A/, A-, Annexure, Exhibit, Document and custom prefixes.
- Page numbering for all pages, excluding covers, documents only, or from a physical page.
- Six colour themes and Formal, Corporate and Minimal layouts.
- Optional visible PNG/JPG stamp or signature and text stamp.
- First/last/all document page and custom-page stamping.
- Non-blocking warnings for duplicate annexures, blank particulars and long subjects.
- Updated manifest and verification guidance.

VERIFIED
- Long subject retained through its final sentence.
- Long particulars retained through the word "Prayer" and beyond.
- Single-volume 18-page generation with documents-only numbering 1-16.
- Multi-volume split at 10 physical pages with continuous numbering 1-16.
- Split-document Part 1/Part 2 mapping.
- Internal/remote annotations and PDF bookmarks.
- Portrait and rotated landscape placement.
- Visible stamp/signature placement and text stamp generation.

IMPORTANT
Visible signature/stamp placement is not DSC signing. Retain original digitally signed
source files separately because merging does not preserve cryptographic signature validity.
