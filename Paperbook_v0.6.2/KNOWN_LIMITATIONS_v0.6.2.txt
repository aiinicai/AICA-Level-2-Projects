PROFESSIONAL v0.6.2 LIMITATIONS
================================

1. User-supplied first-page templates are not included yet; they can be added when supplied.
2. Smart justification applies only to the generated cover subject, not uploaded evidence PDFs.
3. Extremely long cover fields may require Small text or shorter cover wording; review the exact preview.
4. Long unbroken text is force-wrapped without adding a hyphen so the original searchable text is retained.
5. Drag-and-drop depends on browser support; Up/Down buttons remain available.
6. The reference checker is a drafting aid; final citations require manual legal review.
7. Visible signature/stamp placement is not DSC signing. Preserve unchanged signed originals.
8. The MB guide warns after generation and does not guarantee size-based splitting.
9. Compression does not aggressively downsample scans or certify DPI/grayscale.
10. Portal requirements may change; confirm the live filing requirements before submission.
11. Cross-volume links, forms, annotations and copied signed pages require Adobe Reader verification.
12. Large scanned cases remain dependent on available browser memory.
