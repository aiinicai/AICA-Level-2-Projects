SME GSTAT AppealKit - Intelligent v0.5.0
Release date: 06 August 2026

ADDED
- Optional reversible page editor with thumbnail view, rotate, reorder, remove/restore, Undo and Reset.
- Original page-to-output page mapping in the audit manifest.
- Optional document details with plain examples and user-created columns.
- Editable chronology with add/delete/reorder rows, custom columns and CSV export.
- Optional inclusion of chronology in the master index and continuous pagination.
- Optional issue field for multiple issue tags.
- Offline appeal-reference checking for DOCX, searchable PDF and TXT.
- Exact scrollable PDF preview, volume selector and preview-out-of-date warning.
- Current preview bytes are reused in the final ZIP when nothing has changed.
- Detection warning and separate retention of original digitally signed PDFs.
- Local PDF.js renderer for page thumbnails and searchable-PDF text extraction.

SAFETY
- Original uploaded files are never rewritten by page editing.
- “Remove page” means exclude from paper book; Restore and Reset remain available.
- Visible signature/stamp remains an image only and is not represented as a DSC.
- Appeal-reference results are suggestions only; the original draft is never changed.
- Forms, annotations and signed PDFs receive a plain-language warning before editing.
