SME GSTAT AppealKit - Professional v0.6.1
===========================================

1. FIRST-PAGE PRESENTATION
- Added Small, Normal and Large first-page text settings.
- Case-title/subject paragraphs are justified like a Word paragraph.
- The final subject line remains naturally left aligned.
- Long subjects continue to reduce safely to fit without intentional truncation.

2. DATE PRINTING
- HTML date input is converted from YYYY-MM-DD to DD-MMM-YYYY in generated PDFs.
- Example: 2026-08-06 prints as 06-Aug-2026.

3. DOCUMENT REARRANGEMENT
- Added a visible drag handle to every document row.
- Drop above or below another row to choose the exact new position.
- Up and Down buttons remain as an accessible alternative.
- Automatic numbering refreshes after a drag when enabled.

4. TWO-STAGE ANNEXURE NUMBERING
- New default pattern: 1, 2, 3 followed by A1, A2, A3.
- The number of main documents before the A-series is editable.
- Existing single-series prefixes, Arabic, alphabetic and Roman options remain available.

5. ANNEXURE REFERENCE CHECKER
- Added Anx abbreviation and compact references such as Anx12 and A12.
- Captures up to four digits and preserves the complete matched reference.
- Prevents Anx1 from being reported as the match inside Anx12.
- Named Annexure 12 references can resolve to an available A12 label.

No cover-template uploader was added in this release; supplied templates can be added in a later template pack.
