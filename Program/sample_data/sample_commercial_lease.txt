COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made and executed on this 1st day of April 2024 at Bangalore, Karnataka,

BY AND BETWEEN:
Horizon Realty & Commercial Spaces Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at Prestige Cyber Towers, Outer Ring Road, Bangalore - 560103, represented by its Authorised Signatory, Mr. Ramesh Kumar (hereinafter referred to as the "Lessor", which expression shall unless repugnant to the context include its successors and assigns) of the FIRST PART;

AND

Apex Tech Solutions India Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its corporate office at Tech Boulevard, Sector 62, Noida - 201309, represented by its Director, Ms. Priya Sharma (hereinafter referred to as the "Lessee", which expression shall unless repugnant to the context include its successors and permitted assigns) of the SECOND PART.

WHEREAS:
A. The Lessor is the absolute owner of the commercial property situated at Unit No. 402, 4th Floor, Tech Park One, Whitefield, Bangalore, measuring an aggregate super built-up area of 5,000 sq. ft. (hereinafter referred to as the "Demised Premises").
B. The Lessee desires to take on lease the Demised Premises for commercial office purposes, and the Lessor has agreed to lease out the Demised Premises on the terms and conditions stipulated herein.

NOW THEREFORE THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. LEASE COMMENCEMENT AND TERM
1.1 The lease duration of the Demised Premises shall be for a period of 60 months (5 years) commencing with effect from 1st April 2024 (the "Commencement Date") and expiring on 31st March 2029.
1.2 The physical possession of the Demised Premises has been handed over to the Lessee on 01-04-2024 for fit-out and commercial occupation.

2. RENT AND PAYMENT CONVENTION
2.1 Monthly Rent: The Lessee agrees to pay to the Lessor a monthly fixed rent of Rs. 150,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand only).
2.2 Payment Timing: The monthly rent shall be payable in advance on or before the 7th day of each calendar month into the designated bank account of the Lessor.
2.3 Rent-Free Period: The Lessor grants a rent-free period of 2 months from 1st April 2024 to 31st May 2024 for fit-outs and interior furnishings. Rent payments shall start from 1st June 2024.

3. RENT ESCALATION
3.1 The monthly rent payable under Clause 2.1 shall escalate by 5% every 12 months on a compounded basis.

4. COMMON AREA MAINTENANCE (CAM) & NON-LEASE CHARGES
4.1 The Lessee shall pay Common Area Maintenance (CAM) charges of Rs. 25,000/- per month directly to the facility management agency for 24/7 security, power backup, and common area upkeep.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
5.1 The Lessee has paid an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 600,000/- (Rupees Six Lakhs only) on signing, refundable within 15 days of vacant handover.

6. INITIAL DIRECT COSTS & RESTORATION OBLIGATION
6.1 The Lessee has incurred initial direct costs of Rs. 75,000/- towards lease registration, stamp duty, and legal documentation.
6.2 The Lessee covenants to restore the Demised Premises to original bare shell condition on lease termination. The estimated restoration and dismantling obligation is Rs. 100,000/-.

7. RENEWAL AND TERMINATION
7.1 Lock-in Period: Both parties agree to a lock-in period of 36 months from commencement.
7.2 Early termination by Lessee after lock-in requires 3 months written notice.
7.3 Option to Renew: The Lessee holds an option to extend the lease for an additional 24 months subject to mutual agreement on market rental rates.

8. GOVERNING LAW & ARBITRATION
8.1 This agreement is governed by the laws of India and subject to arbitration in Bangalore.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Lease Agreement on the date first above written.
