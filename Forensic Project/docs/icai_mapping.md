# ICAI Forensic Accounting Standards & Study Material Grounding Matrix

This document maps all 44 deterministic rules and statistical tests in the Red Flag Engine directly to the **ICAI (Institute of Chartered Accountants of India) Board of Studies** study material on Forensic Accounting (Chapters 3, 4, and 6) and ICAI Forensic Accounting and Investigation Standards (FAIS).

---

## 1. Statutory and Professional Standards Grounding

- **Companies Act, 2013**:
  - Section 447 (Punishment for Fraud)
  - Section 143(12) & Rule 13 (Reporting of Fraud by Auditor to Central Government)
  - Section 36 (Punishment for fraudulently inducing persons to invest money)
  - Section 185 / 186 (Loans & Investments to Related Parties)
  - Section 189 (Register of Contracts and Arrangements in which Directors are Interested)
- **Standard on Auditing (SA) 240**: *The Auditor's Responsibilities Relating to Fraud in an Audit of Financial Statements*
- **ICAI Forensic Accounting and Investigation Standards (FAIS)**:
  - FAIS 130 (*Predication*)
  - FAIS 140 (*Professional Skepticism*)
  - FAIS 210 / 220 (*Engagement and Documentation*)
- **CARO 2020**: Reporting on working capital borrowings, related party transactions, statutory dues, and revaluation of assets.
- **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA)**: Replaced the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 from 1 July 2024; electronic records admissibility (Section 61/63).
