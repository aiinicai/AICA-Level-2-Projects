# AI CFO Enterprise Platform
### CFO Intelligence & Decision Support System - AI-Powered Virtual CFO
**AICA Level 2 capstone (ICAI)** | Demo company: *Gulf Equipment Trading LLC* (fictional, OMR) | Python + Streamlit

A CFO-grade, navy-and-white analytics platform. You upload a trial balance, budget, sub-ledgers and documents; five independent AI agents coordinated by a Supervisor investigate the numbers, cite the company's own policies, check invoices, forecast cash and produce a board pack.

![Dashboard](docs/screenshots/01_dashboard.png)

## The six capabilities
| # | Capability | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Interactive executive dashboard with business-unit analysis (BU P&L, shared-cost allocation, BU budget variance, return on assets, DSO) | Executive Dashboard, Business Units |
| 2 | **Five genuinely independent agents** (Performance, Liquidity, Forecast & Scenario, Risk & Compliance, Document Intelligence) + **Supervisor** with routing, parallel execution, cross-agent checks, audit trail | AI CFO |
| 3 | **RAG** over 8 financial policies (BM25, heading chunks, clause citations such as `[POL-03 - 2. Bank covenants]`); policy thresholds drive the agents' tests; upload your own policies | Policy Knowledge |
| 4 | **OCR** of invoices (PDF text, scanned PNG/JPG) and bank statements; 8 validation checks incl. duplicate, VAT, delegation of authority, vendor master; bank reconciliation | Document Intelligence |
| 5 | **Predictive analytics**: payment-delay ML model, 13-week cash forecast, Base/Upside/Downside/Stress scenarios, Monte Carlo breach probability | Cash & Scenarios |
| 6 | **Automated board pack**: 14-slide PowerPoint, Word management report, Excel workbook | Board Pack & Reports |

Design principle: **Python calculates, AI explains.** All figures are computed deterministically; language models only narrate and synthesise from computed facts and retrieved policy text. No API key is needed: free keyless service, your own key, or a fully offline analyst (automatic fallback).

## Run
```
pip install -r requirements.txt
streamlit run app.py
```
or double-click `run_app.bat` (Windows) / `./run_app.sh`. Tests: `pip install -r requirements-dev.txt && pytest -q` (22 tests).

Sample data is regenerated by `python data_generator/generate_sample_data.py` and `python data_generator/generate_documents.py` (needs reportlab).

## Limitations (honest notes)
- Sample data is synthetic; findings on it are illustrative. Facility limit is an assumption (drawn + OMR 600k) editable in Data & Settings.
- OCR accuracy on poor scans depends on image quality; every extracted figure is validated, not trusted.
- Predictive models are trained on the synthetic payment history; treat probabilities as demonstrations of method.
- Agents and BU views analyse the latest period selected.
