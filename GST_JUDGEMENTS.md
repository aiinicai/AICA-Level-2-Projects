# GST Notice Judgement Advisor (404+ SC & HC Judgements)

**Live App:** [https://singlaamarnath.ai.studio/](https://singlaamarnath.ai.studio/)  
**Curated By:** CA Amar Nath Singla

## Overview
**GST Notice Judgement Advisor** is a legal and compliance research application built in Google AI Studio featuring **404+ Supreme Court and High Court precedents** across major Goods and Services Tax (GST) provisions—including Sections 75(4), 169, 73, 74, 16(4), 129/130, 83, 67/70, 17(5), 50, 107, and 128A. It helps tax practitioners and taxpayers analyze GST notices, compare favorable versus adverse rulings, review statutory guardrails, and draft structured responses.

## Use Case & Problem Solved
- **Problem:** Responding to GST notices, show cause notices (`DRC-01`), scrutiny notices (`ASMT-10`), demand orders (`DRC-07`), transit detentions (`MOV-07` / `MOV-09`), or bank attachments (`DRC-22`) requires quickly locating relevant High Court and Supreme Court rulings on natural justice, service defects, limitation, and input tax credit disputes while distinguishing unfavorable precedents.
- **Solution:** Centralizes section-wise case law, notice text analysis, favorable-vs-adverse precedent comparisons, procedural defense checklists, and reply drafting in one interactive workspace—cutting down manual case-law hunting and helping frame clear legal grounds.
- **Target Users:** Chartered accountants, GST practitioners, tax litigators, finance/compliance teams, and business owners handling GST notices and appeals.

## Key Features
- **Section-Wise Judgements & Master Library (404+):** Browse and filter Supreme Court and High Court decisions by GST section, court, or keyword, with legal ratios, citations, and notice applications.
- **GST Notice Input & AI Notice Text Analyzer:** Enter notice details (form type, financial year, disputed amount, allegations) or paste notice text to match relevant precedents and extract key legal issues.
- **Favourable vs. Adverse Matrix:** Compare pro-taxpayer rulings alongside adverse precedents and notes on how to distinguish unfavorable cases on facts or procedure.
- **SCN Draft Reply Builder & Procedural Defense Checklist:** Assemble reply arguments, legal grounds, and statutory reminders (such as personal hearing mandates, notice service rules, and speaking-order principles).
- **Voice Advisor (Live API) & Recent Sessions:** Access voice-assisted legal guidance and reload recent analysis sessions quickly.

## How to Use
1. Open [https://singlaamarnath.ai.studio/](https://singlaamarnath.ai.studio/).
2. Choose a **GST Notice Section** card or reload a recent session.
3. Fill in notice parameters or paste notice paragraphs into the analyzer to view matching judgements and defense grounds.
4. Use the comparison matrix, defense checklist, and reply builder to refine your response.

## Disclaimer
This tool is for legal research and drafting assistance only and does not constitute formal legal representation. Always verify full judgement texts, statutory amendments, CBIC circulars, and current facts before filing any reply, appeal, or writ petition.
