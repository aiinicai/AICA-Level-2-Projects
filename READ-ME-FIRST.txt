UPGRADE WITHOUT LOSING TASKS
1. Download a backup in the OLD app: Settings > Download backup.
2. Stop the old app by pressing Ctrl+C in its command window.
3. Extract the new ZIP into its own NEW folder. Keep the old folder.
4. COPY daylight.db from your OLD Daylight folder into the NEW Daylight-v2 folder.
   The database file sits beside server.py. Copy; do not move or delete it.
   Alternatively, start with an empty new app and import your old JSON backup
   through Settings. Use ONE of these methods to avoid duplicate tasks.
5. Double-click START.bat in the NEW folder.
6. Open http://localhost:8503
7. Add your Google AI Studio key in Settings. Keys are not copied with tasks.

No installations needed. Python's built-in libraries are sufficient.
Do not double-click index.html: use START.bat and the localhost address.
Your original app.py and old Daylight folder are not needed by this version.

HOW THE TICKS WORK
Must do today: ahead of nonmandatory flexible work.
Already delayed: priority ahead of ordinary work; combines with Must do today.
Quick win: an extra boost at 20 minutes or less, stronger on Light days.
It never reduces the entered duration or splits the task.
Skip today: excludes the selected planning date, without changing the deadline.
The task is eligible on other dates. Untick to include it again.
Waiting on someone: excluded until unticked.
Must-do/delayed/quick flags remain until unticked; completion clears must-do and delayed.
A previous Available from date still applies; clear it through pencil > Optional details
or choose Undo earlier postponement under More.

Fixed appointments keep their times; conflicts are flagged. Priority tasks must still
fit selected focus, locations, activities and time. If they cannot, a warning appears.
Light days defer high-energy work except priority work. Estimated travel time is not
added. Breaks follow flexible tasks where they fit, not fixed appointments.

TIMER
Runs independently of your schedule and does not mark tasks complete.
Pause/resume/reset and switch between focus/break. Switching mode resets the timer.
Closing the panel keeps it running while the app is open. Refreshing resets it.
Wall-clock timing corrects for background-tab delays. A sleeping/closed browser cannot
sound an alert; on resume an open app catches up. Sound is optional and depends on
browser/audio settings. No automatic next session.

DATA AND AI
Task data and last-planned preferences live in daylight.db in this folder.
Backups exclude your API key. Restore appends tasks; importing twice duplicates them.
The key lives in Python memory until shutdown. Blank key field keeps the current key;
Disconnect AI removes it from memory. Connection is verified by a successful extraction.
Notes sent through Organise tasks and the planning date are transmitted to Google.
The app is local, single-user, and not yet publicly hosted. Use one browser tab to
avoid competing edits. Plans reset on browser refresh; saved tasks remain.
