Definitions and data
  • Data: data/index.json lists the months; each month is data/dashboard_data_<Mon-YYYY>.json, written by consolidate.py from the five country files received through the n8n intake workflow, the FY26 budget and the FX assumptions. Load month files opens one or more dashboard_data JSON files (select several at once) on this device only.
  • Headcount is the population active at month-end. Monthly cost = internal CTC ÷ 12 plus external bill rate × billable units (160 hours, 20 days or 1 month), converted to INR at the month's FX rate.
  • Budget is at the same FX rates, so variances carry no currency effect. Volume effect = HC variance × budget cost per head; rate / mix effect = (actual − budget cost per head) × actual HC. MoM = change against the previous month loaded.
  • Projects: internal employees with hire type "Project" and consultants with engagement type "Project", active at month-end. "Joined in month" counts those whose hire date or contract start falls in the month. There is no project budget in the sample, so the Projects page shows actuals and trend only.
  • All names and figures are fictitious sample data for the ICAI AICA Level 2 capstone.
