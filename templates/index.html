PHASE 1 · INGESTION & INFLOW

Automated Contract Note Ingestion

Stage A (needs internet) fetches broker PDFs from Gmail into the watch folder below. Stage B (fully offline) then parses and posts them.

This client's contract notes, processed/errors history and logs are kept separate from every other client, in their own subfolder.

Most brokers password-protect the contract note PDF with the client's own PAN. Each file is tried against every password here until one opens it — add one per client you file for.

GMAIL FILTER & WATCHER SETTINGS

Which emails Stage A should look at

Contract notes reach a practice's inbox two ways: directly from the broker, or forwarded by a client from their own mailbox. Pick a strategy so Stage A finds the right messages before Stage B identifies the actual broker from the note itself.

Used when the strategy is "Client Forwards" or "All".

A shorter window is faster and less likely to re-surface old correspondence on a large mailbox. Full inbox has no limit but can be slow the first time.
