# Goods Receipt Note (GRN) Management System

## Overview

The **Goods Receipt Note (GRN) Management System** is a mobile-friendly Progressive Web Application (PWA) developed using **Google AI Studio** to digitize and simplify the process of recording goods received at a factory gate.

The application is primarily designed for **marble, granite, and natural stone processing factories**, where large stone blocks and other materials are received regularly.

It replaces traditional handwritten GRN registers with a digital system, helping improve accuracy, reduce paperwork, and maintain organized records of incoming materials.

## Key Features

- **Hindi Language Interface:** Designed for factory gatekeepers and workers who are more comfortable using Hindi.
- **Mobile-Friendly PWA:** Suitable for smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers.
- **Digital GRN Creation:** Record incoming goods electronically instead of using handwritten receipts.
- **Goods Receipt Details:** Capture relevant information such as receipt date, supplier, vehicle number, material description, quantity, and other GRN particulars.
- **Marble and Stone Block Recording:** Designed to accommodate goods receipt transactions involving marble, granite, and stone blocks.
- **Digital GRN Generation:** Generate structured Goods Receipt Notes based on the entered information.
- **Printing and Recordkeeping:** Designed to support printable GRNs and electronic recordkeeping.
- **Simple User Interface:** Easy-to-understand forms and controls suitable for workers with limited computer knowledge.
- **Reduced Manual Errors:** Standardized data-entry fields help improve consistency and accuracy.

## Objective

The primary objective of this application is to **digitize the factory gate goods receiving process** and create a simple, efficient, and user-friendly GRN system.

The application aims to:

1. Eliminate dependency on handwritten GRN registers.
2. Improve the accuracy of goods receipt records.
3. Reduce the time required to prepare GRNs.
4. Make GRN entry accessible to Hindi-speaking factory workers.
5. Improve the traceability of incoming raw materials.
6. Support future integration with inventory and stock management systems.

## Intended Users

- Factory gatekeepers
- Goods receiving personnel
- Storekeepers
- Factory supervisors
- Inventory and stock management teams
- Factory owners and management

## Typical Workflow

1. Open the GRN application on a mobile phone or other device.
2. Enter the required details of goods received at the factory gate.
3. Record supplier, vehicle, material, quantity, and other relevant information.
4. Review the entered details for accuracy.
5. Generate a digital Goods Receipt Note.
6. Print or retain the GRN electronically, where supported.
