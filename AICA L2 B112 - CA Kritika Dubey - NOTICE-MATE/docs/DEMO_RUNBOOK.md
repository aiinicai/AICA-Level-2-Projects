# Fictional Demo Runbook

## Before recording

- Use a fresh fictional case only. Do not show a real client's name, PAN, documents, e-mail, browser history, desktop files or account details.
- Confirm the app is running from this capstone folder and that the visible sample records are marked fictional.
- The API/model is not configured in this package. Do not imply that the displayed offline reply was generated by AI.
- Record the screen and your own face as required by the course instructions. Keep the video link accessible to the reviewers as requested by the course.

## Suggested walkthrough

1. Introduce the problem and explain that the project is an educational prototype for organising a notice-response workflow.
2. Show upload of the fictional multi-issue notice and several matching fictional records from `sample-files/complex_future_faceless_test/`.
3. Show the extracted notice details and explain that suggested values must be checked.
4. Show issue-wise document matches and identify at least one missing or conflicting item. Explain that a match is a suggestion, not proof.
5. Show a reconciliation screen, if used, and explain that internal agreement does not prove a transaction's source or genuineness.
6. Show the drafting screen and state clearly that AI is not configured in this capstone copy. Point out the brief template and any CA confirmation markers as limitations.
7. Explain the intended optional AI design from `AI_PROMPT_AND_LIMITS.md`: retrieval of relevant passages, evidence-linked paragraph drafting, redaction preview and explicit approval.
8. Close by summarising privacy boundaries, professional review requirements and the next improvements.

## Do not claim

- That the software gives legal or tax advice, verifies a lender's capacity, proves a source of funds, or files a reply.
- That the current short offline reply is a complete or validated AI-generated answer.
- That a legal citation or deadline is current unless it has been checked against the official source for the relevant year.
