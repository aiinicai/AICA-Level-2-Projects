# Ten evaluation points — Easy AI Procura
**Submitted by:** Parveen Bajaj | **Course:** AICA Level 2

## 1. Define the business problem
Disconnected purchase orders, physical receipts and invoices can lead to payment for unordered, undelivered, damaged or overpriced goods. This project addresses the control gap before invoice approval.

## 2. State the intended achievement
Connect LPO → GRN → invoice matching so purchasing, stores and finance can work from linked records. The aim is better approval evidence, fewer unmatched invoices and clearer responsibility, not automated payment execution.

## 3. Explain the problem-first AI journey
As reported by the project author, the problem was discussed with Perplexity to propose a structure, suitable solutions, alternative approaches and code. Several conversations refined the design before Lovable was used for AI-assisted “vibe coding”. The Perplexity conversation itself is not included or independently verified in this package; the author can add their original chat evidence if required.

## 4. Explain why Lovable was selected
Natural-language instructions and immediate visual review enabled an accounting professional to translate business requirements into a working application. Integrated hosting, login and data storage reduced assembly work. AI-generated suggestions still required review, tests and security checks.

## 5. Strengthen accountability
Maker–checker workflows, controlled master data and approval history support separation of duties and traceability. These are internal-control features, not a claim of ISO certification or guaranteed regulatory compliance. Deployment controls require further review.

## 6. Complement an unavailable or inflexible ERP
The tool can operate independently or alongside an ERP that is difficult to customise. Excel/CSV master imports and GRN exports provide file exchange, including units of measurement. They are not certified or live QuickBooks, Tally or ERP integrations.

## 7. Make daily work easier
Receipt entry reuses purchase-order details. Users can see shortages, damaged or wrong items and invoice price differences without maintaining multiple disconnected spreadsheets. Shared registers and approval queues can reduce repeated entry and follow-up.

## 8. Improve visibility and analysis
Dashboards and graphs show recorded spend and document status. AI Insights assist analysis of supplier concentration, price variances, approval delays and trends. Accuracy depends on source data; AI supports rather than replaces professional judgement.

## 9. Improve access and communication
Mobile/desktop access and installation improve convenience. Linked, approved supplier contacts have scoped document access. Offline support is limited to previously visited screens; current records and approvals need connectivity. Automatic app-sent delivery notices remain future work pending sender-domain setup.

## 10. Measure success and extend responsibly
Track invoices approved against approved GRNs each week, approval turnaround time, unmatched invoices and delivery discrepancies. Future work includes budget checks, approval thresholds, receipt attachments, supplier scoring, direct integrations and Python/R analysis. No quantified productivity or cost savings are claimed without measured evidence.

## Conclusion
Easy AI Procura demonstrates how accounting knowledge and AI-assisted development can turn a practical procurement-control problem into a working web application. It links orders, receipts and invoices and offers a flexible companion where ERP workflows are unavailable or difficult to customise.
