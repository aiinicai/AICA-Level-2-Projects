Welcome to the MSME Debtors Management & Statutory Interest Calculator!
This software operates as a high-performance Progressive Web Application (PWA)
and can be installed permanently on Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS systems.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DIRECT ACCESS URL:
https://msme-debtors-manager.ai.studio
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

METHOD 1: INSTANT 1-CLICK LAUNCHER (INCLUDED IN THIS ZIP)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* WINDOWS USERS:
  Double-click "Launch_MSME_Debtors_Windows.bat" or "MSME_Debtors_Management.url".
  It will automatically open the app in full standalone windowed mode.

* MAC / LINUX USERS:
  Double-click "Launch_MSME_Debtors_Mac.command".

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2: PERMANENT SYSTEM INSTALLATION (STANDALONE DESKTOP APP)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once the app is open in your browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave):

1. Click the glowing "Install App" button in the top navigation bar.
   OR
2. Click the Install Icon (Computer with down-arrow) in your browser's address bar.
3. Click "Install" when prompted.

Result:
- An app icon will be pinned to your Desktop and Start Menu.
- Runs in its own dedicated, borderless desktop window without address bar tabs.
- Full offline caching enabled for ledger data.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 3: MOBILE INSTALLATION (ANDROID / IPHONE)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* ANDROID:
  1. Open the URL in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".

* IPHONE / IPAD (iOS):
  1. Open the URL in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up) at bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" in top-right.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NEED AN ENTERPRISE .EXE / .MSI INSTALLER?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If your organization requires a traditional Windows .msi / .exe installer file:
1. Visit Microsoft's official PWA Builder: https://www.pwabuilder.com
2. Paste the App URL: https://msme-debtors-manager.ai.studio
3. Click "Package for Windows" to download a signed .msix / .exe installer.
