📖 User Guide & Automated Test Suite

Core Workflow Guide

  1. Entity Setup: Configure Company Name, Financial Year, Comparative Period, Currency, and Rounding Unit in Entity & Period Setup tab.
  2. TB Import: Upload your Tally export file (Excel, CSV, or XML) or click Load Demo Sample Data.
  3. Ledger Mapping: Review auto-mapped ledgers. Pay attention to items marked CA Review Required and update classification as needed.
  4. Reconciliation: Check the Exceptions & Audit Checklist tab to confirm that Balance Sheet tallies ($Assets = Equity + Liabilities$).
  5. Export Outputs: Download audit working papers in Excel, Word, or PDF.

Data Required Checklist

  • ✅ Tally Trial Balance (Current FY & Comparative FY)
  • ✅ Shareholding pattern (> 5% shareholders & promoters shareholding)
  • ✅ Fixed Assets Addition/Deletion register for PPE Schedule
  • ✅ MSME Creditors classification confirmation
  • ✅ Ageing breakdown for Sundry Debtors and Creditors
