ℹ️ About ClientLedger India
ClientLedger India v3.0
Fully local, browser-based Client Management System with Indian compliance (Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN, TAN, DIN) and portal quick-login for GST, TRACES & Income Tax portals.
All data is stored only in this browser's IndexedDB. No server. No internet required. Take regular backups to protect your data.

🚀 GST Quick-Check Workflow

After logging in and checking their return status, record GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B dates here — then move to the next client automatically. A CA can check 20–30 clients in under 15 minutes this way.
Credentials stored · Portal-assisted

Step 3: Log in to the portal, verify filing status, return here and switch to the Update Status tab. Step 4: Record the filed dates in the status panel. All data stays in your browser only — no API, no internet call.

📑 GSTR-3B Scrape
Extract GSTR-3B data directly from live portal (no PDF) — supports Monthly & QRMP filers
📌
How it works: Step 1: Select Client + Financial Year + tick "Force re-scrape" if you want to overwrite cached data, then click ▶ Start Scrape. Step 2: Bot launches a browser, logs in to the GST portal — enter CAPTCHA here if prompted; approve OTP on your phone. 🏦 TDS / TCS Credit Received — Download
Download TDS & TCS Credit Received JSON — auto-detects Filed (DOWNLOAD) vs Unfiled (PREPARE OFFLINE), multi-sweep like GSTR-1
📌
How it works: Step 1: Select Client + Financial Year (optional: specific month), then click ▶ Start Download. Step 2: Bot opens the GST portal → Services → Returns → TDS & TCS Credit Received (returns2/auth/comptds). Enter CAPTCHA here if prompted; approve OTP on your phone. Step 3 (Sweep 1): For each month → selects FY + Month → SEARCH. If Filed: DOWNLOAD → Offline page → JSON. If Not Filed: PREPARE OFFLINE → Offline page → JSON. Quick 3-second check per month. Step 4 (Sweep 2): Revisits any pending months, waiting up to 5 minutes each for file generation. Step 5: Once complete, click 📊 Export Excel to convert all downloaded JSON files into a structured 5-sheet workbook.
⚠ The browser window opens automatically — do not close it during the process.
