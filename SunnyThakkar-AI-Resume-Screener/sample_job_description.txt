Job Title: Data Analyst
Company: Horizon Retail Group (Muscat, Oman)

About the role
We are looking for a Data Analyst to turn sales and customer data into clear insights for our management team.

Responsibilities
- Collect, clean and analyse sales, inventory and customer data
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI or Tableau
- Write SQL queries to pull data from our data warehouse
- Use Python (pandas, NumPy) for data analysis and automation
- Run statistical analysis and A/B testing on promotions
- Build simple forecasting and predictive models (machine learning is a plus)
- Present findings to stakeholders in clear reports and presentations

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Computer Science, Business or similar
- 3+ years of experience as a data analyst
- Strong SQL, Excel (pivot tables, VLOOKUP) and Python
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Good communication skills and fluent English; Arabic is a plus
- Familiar with Git and agile ways of working
