# Legal-source notes

Source notes checked **9 November 2026**. These are limited drafting prompts, not an exhaustive legal opinion. The app does not fetch updates. Check current amendments, the relevant tax period, SGST/IGST law, notifications, circulars and binding decisions before use on a live matter.

| Source | What it supports in this prototype |
|---|---|
| [India Code - CGST Act, 2017](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/15689?locale=en) | Act structure and section topics: 16/17 (ITC), 37/38/39 (returns/supply information), 47 (late fee), 50 (interest), 61 (scrutiny), 73/74/74A (demand proceedings). Not a substitute for reading each applicable provision. |
| [CBIC - section 16](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter5/section16_v1.00.html) | ITC conditions; invoice possession alone does not establish eligibility. The source records section 16(2)(aa) commencement from 1 January 2022. The app asks for period-specific review rather than automatically denying a difference. |
| [CBIC - section 17](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter5/section17_v1.00.html) | Blocked-credit categories and exceptions, and the distinction from business/non-business or exempt-supply apportionment. The app does not decide whether an exception applies. |
| [CBIC - section 9](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section9_v1.00.html) | Notified reverse-charge categories and specified recipients/supplies. The app does not claim every unregistered purchase attracts RCM. |
| [India Code - IGST Act, 2017](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2251?view_type=browse) | IGST Act structure, including section 5 (levy and collection). Where IGST is involved, verify the actual provision and notification separately. |
| [CBIC - section 61](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter12/section61_v1.00.html) | Scrutiny of returns and seeking explanations. |
| [CBIC - rule 99](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter11/rule99_v1.00.html) | ASMT-10 scrutiny communication, explanation in ASMT-11, and ASMT-12 if accepted. Service/extension details matter; no automatic calendar deadline is generated. |
| [CBIC - rule 142](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter18/rule142_v1.00.html) | DRC-01 is the summary accompanying a notice. DRC-01A Part A is a pre-notice communication; Part B submissions are addressed by sub-rule (2A). Sub-rule (4) addresses replies in DRC-06. |
| [CBIC - section 73](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter15/section73_v1.00.html) | Section 73 text restricts its demand determination to periods up to FY 2023-24. |
| [CBIC - section 74A](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter15/Section74A_v1.00.html) | Demand determination for FY 2024-25 onward. The app deliberately avoids a single universal response/payment/penalty period. |

## Boundaries

- The curated notes paraphrase limited principles and link to sources; they are not verbatim legislation or case-specific advice.
- No statutory rates, fee caps, waivers, interest figures, limitation periods or payment options are computed.
- Sample notice dates, rates implicit in amounts and allegations are fictional and do not establish a legal liability or applicable notification.
- No automated legal database, GST portal lookup, citation generator or case-law search is present.
