Definitions and data
  • Data: data/dashboard_data.json, written by consolidate.py from the five country files received through the n8n intake workflow, the FY26 budget and the FX assumptions. Replace this file each month, or use Load month file to view another month on this device.
  • Headcount is the population active at month-end. Monthly cost = internal CTC ÷ 12 plus external bill rate × billable units (160 hours, 20 days or 1 month), converted to INR at the month's FX rate.
  • Budget is at the same FX rates, so variances carry no currency effect. Volume effect = HC variance × budget cost per head; rate / mix effect = (actual − budget cost per head) × actual HC.
  • All names and figures are fictitious sample data for the ICAI AICA Level 2 capstone.
