Understanding Risk Metrics & Portfolio Impact

1. Annualised Volatility Price Fluctuations

Measures the intensity of price swings (ups and downs) in your portfolio over a 1-year horizon, calculated via standard deviation of monthly returns.

Benchmarking: Nifty 50 is typically 14% - 16%.
Your Portfolio: 22.6% (Elevated risk).
Impact on your Corpus: On a ₹3,00,000 portfolio, 22.6% volatility means your capital may easily swing by ±₹67,800 in a typical market year due to 100% equity and heavy banking concentration.
2. Portfolio Sharpe Ratio Risk vs Reward

Evaluates return generated per unit of risk taken above the risk-free rate (6.0% benchmark return from risk-free sovereign debt/FD).

Scale: <1.0 = Poor | 1.0 - 1.5 = Good | >1.5 = Stellar
Your Portfolio: 1.06 (Marginal efficiency).
Impact on your Corpus: While HDFC (1.21) and Parag Parikh (1.19) generate healthy risk-adjusted returns, Kotak Regular (0.79) acts as an efficiency drag due to distributor commission and higher variance.
3. Max Historical Drawdown Worst-Case Drop

Measures the largest peak-to-trough decline experienced by your portfolio before reaching a new high. Reveals the worst historical dip.

Safe Target: Within -12% to -15%.
Your Portfolio: -19.8% peak-to-trough drop.
Impact on your Corpus: During severe market corrections, your ₹3,00,000 corpus would have temporarily fallen to ₹2,40,600 (-₹59,400). Kotak fell hardest (-24.2%) while Parag Parikh cushioned downside (-17.4%).
Actionable Portfolio Fix: Your portfolio's high volatility (22.6%) and steep drawdown (-19.8%) occur because all 3 funds are 100% Flexi Cap equities sharing a 50.5% stock overlap. Adding non-correlated assets (e.g., a Dynamic Asset Allocation fund or Direct plan rebalancing) significantly improves your Sharpe ratio while insulating against severe market corrections.

MF X-RAY V1.0

AI-Powered Mutual Fund Portfolio Intelligence & Risk Analysis System

ICAI AI Level 2 Capstone Project

Core Mission: An educational and analytical portfolio-intelligence tool engineered to look through the mutual-fund wrapper. Investors often assume that holding multiple mutual funds provides diversification; however, MF X-Ray demonstrates that significant overlap in underlying companies and sectoral concentration can create hidden risk.

Key Methodologies:

• Effective Look-Through Exposure: Calculates your net percentage ownership in each stock across all funds in your portfolio, weighted by your invested capital.

• Pairwise Overlap: Measures common stock holdings between any two funds by summing the minimum shared weight of each identical company.

• Portfolio Health Score: Transparent 0–100 score built from 6 independent components (Diversification 20, Overlap 20, Stock Concentration 20, Sector Concentration 15, Volatility 15, Max Drawdown 10).

Statutory Disclaimer: MF X-Ray is an educational and analytical portfolio-intelligence tool. It is not investment advice and does not constitute a recommendation to buy, hold, sell or switch any mutual fund or security. Users should independently evaluate their financial circumstances and objectives before making investment decisions.
