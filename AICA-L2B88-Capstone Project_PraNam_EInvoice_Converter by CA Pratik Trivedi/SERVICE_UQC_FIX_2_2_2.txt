SERVICE INVOICE UQC FIX – v2.2.2

For service supplies (IsServc=Y):
- UQC is NOT mandatory.
- Quantity is NOT mandatory.
- No GST UQC RED error is raised merely because UQC is absent.
- The converter does not force OTHERS as a UQC.
- Qty/FreeQty/Unit are omitted from generated JSON for service items.
- Goods continue to require applicable quantity/UQC validation.
