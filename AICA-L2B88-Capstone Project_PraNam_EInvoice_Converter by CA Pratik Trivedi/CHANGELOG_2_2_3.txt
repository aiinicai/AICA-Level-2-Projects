PraNam E-Invoice Converter v2.2.3

Changes in this build:
1. Renamed application to PraNam E-Invoice Converter.
2. Reworked main dashboard with a modern premium glass-style card layout.
3. Reworked Review & Correct window into a central correction workspace.
4. Current RED/AMBER issues are always visible inside the correction window.
5. Clicking an issue jumps to/highlights the related field or item.
6. Corrections are retained in one authoritative working invoice state.
7. Closing Review & Correct with "Close & keep corrections" saves the current values and revalidates them.
8. Main screen is synchronized immediately after correction-window changes.
9. Removed repeated manual-correction notes caused by repeated re-checks.
10. UQC dropdown supports keyboard prefix/type-ahead search.
11. Service invoices no longer require quantity/UQC validation; service Qty/UQC are omitted from JSON.
12. Service state is retained through correction/re-check instead of being accidentally converted to goods logic.
13. Service line Qty/UQC are displayed as Not Applicable in the correction grid.
14. Generate remains blocked while RED validation issues exist.
