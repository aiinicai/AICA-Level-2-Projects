# Section 44ADA — Presumptive taxation, specified profession

**Period:** FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27 · Verified 18 September 2026 · [`sources.md`](sources.md)

---

## Why it matters for audit

1. **The first proviso to s. 44AB** (Finance Act 2023) takes a professional who declares under
   s. 44ADA(1) outside s. 44AB — so gross receipts up to ₹75 lakh need no audit where the
   conditions are met, even though s. 44AB(b) itself says ₹50 lakh.
2. **Section 44AB(d)** requires audit where s. 44ADA applies, profit below 50% is declared,
   and total income exceeds the basic exemption. **There is no prior-year condition** — it
   applies in the first year a lower profit is declared.

## Who can use it

A **resident individual or a partnership firm other than an LLP** (e-filing portal, ITR-4
FAQ) — **an HUF cannot** — in a profession referred to in s. 44AA(1): legal, medical,
engineering, architectural, accountancy, technical consultancy, interior decoration, or a
profession notified under it (authorised representative, film artist, company secretary,
information technology).

## The ceiling — Finance Act 2023, s. 17 (Gazette text)

> *"Provided that in case of an assessee where the amount or aggregate of the amounts received
> during the previous year, in cash, does not exceed five per cent. of the total gross receipts
> of such previous year, this sub-section shall have effect as if for the words "fifty lakh
> rupees", the words "seventy-five lakh rupees" had been substituted: Provided further that for
> the purposes of the first proviso, the receipt of amount or aggregate of amounts by a cheque
> drawn on a bank or by a bank draft, which is not account payee, shall be deemed to be the
> receipt in cash."*

## Deemed income

50% of gross receipts.

## The decision, in order

| Gross receipts | Profit ≥ 50%, declared under 44ADA(1) | Profit ≥ 50%, not declared | Profit < 50%, income > exemption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Up to ₹50 lakh | Not applicable | Not applicable | **Applicable — s. 44AB(d)** |
| ₹50–75 lakh, cash ≤ 5% | **Not applicable — first proviso** | Applicable — s. 44AB(b) | Applicable — (b) and (d) |
| ₹50–75 lakh, cash > 5% | Scheme not available | Applicable — s. 44AB(b) | Applicable — s. 44AB(b) |
| Above ₹75 lakh | Scheme not available | Applicable — s. 44AB(b) | Applicable — s. 44AB(b) |

For an HUF, an LLP, a non-resident or a non-specified profession, s. 44ADA does not apply:
only s. 44AB(b) — gross receipts above ₹50 lakh — governs.
