    Overview

    A structured decision-support and documentation system for determining whether tax audit under section 44AB applies to an assessee, and for evidencing that determination in a working paper. It is not a calculator: every conclusion carries the rule it came from, the figures it was measured against, and the points that still need professional judgement.

    How the determination is reached

    1. Facts — the assessee profile and nature of activity are recorded.
    2. Figures — turnover, the receipts and payments split, profit and total income are entered from the books.
    3. Ratios — cash receipt, cash payment and profit percentages are computed.
    4. Tests — each statutory limb is evaluated separately and records its own finding.
    5. Result — applicable, not applicable, or review required.
    6. Documentation — the working paper records all of the above with the provisions relied on.

    AI assistance and human responsibility

    Artificial intelligence was used in building this system for: structuring the decision logic, drafting the plain-language explanations, generating the working paper narrative and document templates, and identifying candidate exception conditions. The prompt library used is published in prompts/.

    Human in the loop. The decision engine is deterministic and makes no AI call at run time. Its thresholds were cross-checked against the Finance Act 2023 (ss. 15–17), the Finance (No. 2) Act 2024 and the Finance Act 2025 as published in the Gazette. Statutory words are quoted only where read from a source. Interpretation, professional judgement, the final conclusion and any filing remain the responsibility of the Chartered Accountant.

    Where these figures come from

    Turnover / gross receipts
    Sales register or fee register for the year. For derivatives and speculative transactions, compute turnover as set out in the ICAI Guidance Note on Tax Audit.
    Amounts received and paid
    Cash book and bank book for the year. The s. 44AB(a) test uses all amounts received and all payments made, not just turnover — refer to the ICAI Guidance Note for inclusions.
    Cash
    Cash book, petty cash and any cash wage register, plus cheques and drafts that are not account payee, which the Act deems to be cash.
