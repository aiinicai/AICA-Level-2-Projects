NRI DTAA claim on mutual fund redemption

Certify. Register. Redeem. Renew.

DTAA is a Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement: a treaty between India and another country on which country taxes an income. This page shows how a non-resident Indian registers a treaty claim with a mutual fund house before redeeming. The sample investor lives in Oman. A complete dummy pack of ten documents is below, in the order it goes to the fund house. All names, folios and dates are made up.

Who can claim

Any non-resident, not only Oman

Form 41 is for any non-resident who claims a treaty benefit. The country can be any that has a treaty with India.

Source: Income Tax Department, Form 41 FAQs

The treaty decides the relief

Each treaty has its own capital gains article. For Oman, Article 15(6) makes gains on other property taxable only in the seller's country of residence. It does not name mutual fund units. The tax adviser and the fund house confirm the fit.

Notification 69/2025 brings in the 2025 protocol. It is in force from 28 May 2025 and applies from the fiscal year that starts on 1 April 2026. It adds Article 27B: a treaty benefit is denied if getting it was one of the principal purposes of the arrangement, unless the benefit fits the treaty's object and purpose. It cuts the tax rate on royalties and technical fees from 15% to 10%. It does not list Article 15, so the capital gains article is as before. The Article 27B test still applies to any claim.

Sources: India–Oman treaty text and Notification 69/2025.

Terms used on this page
Tax residency certificate (TRC)Issued by the tax authority of the country where you are tax resident. It shows your name, status, nationality, tax ID, residence, validity dates and address.
Form 41A declaration you file online on the Income Tax e-portal under section 159(8) of the Income-tax Act 2025. Earlier called Form 10F.
Tax ID number (TIN)The tax number your country of residence gives you. If it gives none, the fund house accepts your Indian passport number with a declaration.
KYC acknowledgementProof that your know-your-customer record is complete and shows non-resident status.
Address proofA utility bill or lease showing your overseas address. One fund house asked for one from the last 3 months.
Request letterYour letter to the fund house listing folios, treaty basis and enclosures, and asking for written confirmation. Sample below.
Fund house declarationA signed declaration in the fund house's own format, covering residence, ownership and no business in India. The fund house sends the blank on request.
RegistrarThe agency that keeps the fund house's investor records and sends confirmations.
FolioYour account number with one fund house. You can hold several.
NRO and NRE accountsIndian bank accounts for non-residents. Proceeds reach NRO first. Moving them to NRE needs the bank's documents.
Frame

Problem

A fund house deducts Indian tax at redemption from a non-resident unless the treaty claim is already registered. Documents sent after a redemption do not reverse tax already deducted. The registration expires with the certificate.

Inputs

Tax residency certificate, Form 41, PAN, passport, KYC acknowledgement, overseas address proof, request letter, fund house declaration.

Process

Prepare the proof. Send one pack to each fund house. Wait for the registrar's confirmation of a treaty period. Redeem inside that period. Renew before it ends.

Outputs

A treaty period registered per folio. Proceeds credited to the NRO account. A reconciled receipt. Funds in the NRE account. A renewal date.

Limits

  • Individuals redeeming Indian mutual funds. Companies are not covered.
  • No tax rates are shown. The treaty and the fund house decide.
  • Fund house formats and lists differ and change.

Preconditions

  • A valid tax residency certificate. Without it, no registration.
  • PAN, and KYC updated as non-resident.
  • An NRO account to receive proceeds.

Worked example: one investor, start to finish

Everything below is a complete dummy pack in the order it goes to the fund house, modelled on a real signed set. Every name, number, folio and date is made up. Each sheet carries a SAMPLE mark so it cannot pass as a real document. Red text marks what you replace with your own.

The sample case

  • Investor: BusyMe (fictional), an Indian citizen living in Muscat, Oman.
  • Holds units with two fund houses: Northstar AMC (folios FOL-1001 and FOL-1002) and Riverbank AMC (folio FOL-2001).
  • Tax year: 2026-27 (1 Apr 2026 to 31 Mar 2027).
  • Certificate period: 05 May 2026 to 31 Oct 2026.
  • Pack sent to Northstar AMC first. Riverbank AMC is not yet done.

Same identifier everywhere

Oman issues individuals no tax ID number. The sample investor therefore states the passport number as the identifier. The same number appears on Form 41, on Annexure G and in the no-TIN declaration. A different number on any one of them invites a query.

The Form 41 FAQs do not say what to enter when a country issues no TIN. Confirm with the fund house or a tax adviser.

How to file it on the portal

  1. Log in to the e-Filing portal. The user ID is the PAN. A non-resident with no PAN uses an NR ID.
  2. Go to e-File, then Income Tax Forms, then File Income Tax Forms.
  3. Open the tab "Forms as per Income Tax Act 2025". Search "Form 41". Click File Now.
  4. Pick the tax year. Click Continue, then Let's Get Started.
  5. Part A: confirm the particulars and Save. They come from My Profile, so complete that first.
  6. Part B: fill the residential information and Save.
  7. Declaration and Verification: fill and Save.
  8. Click Preview and check every line.
  9. Click Proceed To E-Verify, then Yes.
  10. PAN login: verify with EVC or a digital signature. NR ID login: verify with the OTPs sent to your email and mobile.
  11. Note the Transaction ID and the Acknowledgement Receipt Number (ARN). Quote the ARN in the request letter.

Source: Form 41 user manual. The FAQs say the certificate must be valid for the relevant tax year: Form 41 FAQs.

Fund house declaration: what it states

Each fund house sends its own blank format on request. No public link exists. The two formats seen cover these points.

Format A (a short annexure)

  • Folio number and scheme sold.
  • Country where the proceeds are remitted, and that the investor is the beneficiary.
  • The investor is a tax resident of a named country under Article 4 of the treaty, with the tax ID number or its substitute and the tax status.
  • No permanent establishment or fixed base in India.
  • The units are held as an investment, not as stock-in-trade.
  • Where the investor's world income is assessed.
  • An undertaking to pay any Indian tax demand on the remittance and to supply the fund house with documents for any proceedings.
  • Date, period of validity, signature, address.

Format B (a longer declaration on the investor's letterhead)

  • The units are a capital investment, so the gain is a capital gain under the named treaty.
  • The gain is shown under capital gains in the investor's return.
  • The gain is not taxed in the country of residence whether or not remitted there.
  • Days of presence, permanent home, family, place of work and nationality, to support residence.
  • Enclosures: tax residency certificate, Form 41 and address proof.
  • No permanent establishment in India. Beneficial ownership of the income.
  • Proceeds go to an account outside India, or to an NRE or NRO account.
  • Duty to report any change of residence, and an indemnity to the fund house. The fund house may withdraw the benefit without notice.

Check before signing

Format B names "Article 13" of the treaty for capital gains as a general wording. In the India–Oman treaty text, capital gains is Article 15 and Article 13 covers royalties. Use the article of your own country's treaty.

Source: India–Oman treaty text

What one fund house stated in writing

The fund house's client services wrote these points by email. They are its statements, not a reading of the law.

  • Submit documents at its nearest branch or registrar centre. Email cannot change a folio.
  • Once registered, no tax would be deducted on redemption proceeds.
  • Documents sent after a redemption do not reverse tax already deducted.
  • Tax is not deducted from the date of registration under the folio.
  • After the certificate ends, a fresh request is needed.
  • NRO proceeds are no obstacle.
  • Form 41 must be generated on the Income Tax website. Paper forms are not accepted.

A different document

The same fund house's Statement of Additional Information (December 2024) says tax is deducted at redemption without applying treaty rates, and treaty benefit may be claimed in the tax return. It is older and does not match the email.

Confirm the current position in writing before redeeming.
