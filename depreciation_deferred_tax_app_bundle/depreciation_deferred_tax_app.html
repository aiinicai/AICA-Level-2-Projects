Companies Act, 2013 (Schedule II) Rules

  • • WDV Rate Formula: R = [1 - (s/c)^(1/n)] × 100 where s is residual value (default 5%) and n is useful life.
  • • Pro-Rata Additions: Calculated on exact number of days put to use during the acquisition year (Days / 365), then 365/365 in subsequent years.
  • • Extra Shift (NESD): Eligible Plant & Machinery receives 1.5× rate for Double Shift and 2.0× rate for Triple Shift.

Income Tax Act, 1961 (Section 32) Rules

  • • Block of Assets Concept: Individual assets merge into homogeneous percentage blocks (10%, 15%, 30%, 40%).
  • • 180-Day Cut-off: Additions put to use ≥180 days in acquisition FY claim 100% block rate; <180 days claim 50% block rate.
  • • Sec 32(1)(iia) Additional Depr: New Plant & Machinery gets 20% (≥180 days) or 10% (<180 days) extra in year 1 only.

Multi-Year Roll-Forward & Deferred Tax

  • • Year Roll-Forward: Selecting any FY (`2022-23` to `2034-35`) rolls every asset's WDV from its acquisition date up to that FY.
  • • Ind AS 12 (B/S Approach): Temporary Diff = Carrying Amount (Book WDV) − Tax Base (Tax WDV).
  • • Opening DTL Sync: Prior year's Closing Net DTL automatically becomes next year's Opening Net DTL.
