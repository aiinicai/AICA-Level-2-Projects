SUBJECT: ENGAGEMENT LETTER FOR INTERNAL FINANCIAL CONTROLS (IFC) AUDIT

Dear Sirs,

1. OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE OF THE INTERNAL AUDIT
You have requested that we conduct the Internal Audit of Internal Financial Controls (IFC) over Financial Reporting for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.
The audit will encompass:
- Revenue Recognition & Invoicing controls under Ind AS 115
- Credit Notes, Debit Notes, and Accounts Receivable DSO monitoring
- Review of Delegated Authorities (DOA) and ERP automated approval gates

2. MANAGEMENT'S RESPONSIBILITY
The conduct of the internal audit is based on the premise that management and governance acknowledge and understand their responsibility for:
a) The design, implementation, and maintenance of adequate internal financial controls.
b) Providing our engagement team with full access to all records, documents, systems, and personnel.

3. REPORTING AND DELIVERABLES
At the conclusion of our audit fieldwork, we will submit:
a) Detailed Risk Control Matrix (RCM) evaluation.
b) Internal Audit Findings and Agreed Action Plans (Observation Sheets).
c) Final Consolidated Working Paper Repository vaulted under AuditVault archival standards.
