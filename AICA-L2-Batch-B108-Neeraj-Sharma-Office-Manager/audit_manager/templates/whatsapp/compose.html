How sending works
WhatsApp: 1) tick the clients, 2) choose a template or write, 3) press Prepare. 4) On the next screen press Open chat for each person; WhatsApp opens with the message typed. 5) Press Send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp must be signed in on this PC.
Email: first fill in Settings > Email (SMTP) once and press Send test email. Then choose Email here, give a subject, tick the clients and press Send. Each client gets their own email from your firm's address. You can also pick a later date and time.
