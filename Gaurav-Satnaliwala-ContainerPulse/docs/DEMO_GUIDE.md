# ContainerPulse Demo Guide

Use only fictional, redacted, or explicitly authorized container information
during the demonstration.

1. **Open ContainerPulse**  
   Double-click `run_containerpulse.bat` and open the local Streamlit page.

2. **Show the sample Excel**  
   Open `sample_data/sample_containers.xlsx` and point out the two required
   columns: Container Number and Shipping Line. The included values are dummy.

3. **Upload Excel**  
   Upload a prepared, authorized demonstration workbook.

4. **Click Track All Containers**  
   Show the progress indicator and per-container processing.

5. **Show carrier-browser automation**  
   Keep **Show carrier browser** enabled so the audience can observe the public
   carrier page interaction. Do not proceed through CAPTCHA or login prompts.

6. **Show the results dashboard**  
   Explain Total, In Transit, Arrived / Completed, and Tracking Errors, followed
   by status, location, vessel, ETA, event, and checked time.

7. **Show Attention Required**  
   Point out warning-highlighted rows and the Tracking Errors section when a
   carrier fails, blocks access, or does not support a container.

8. **Download updated Excel**  
   Download and open the current-run report.

9. **Briefly show Shipping Line Registry**  
   Open **Admin / Shipping Line Registry**, show MSC and Maersk as Ready, and
   explain the Not Configured / Setup Failed / Ready lifecycle for new carriers.

Before recording or taking screenshots, confirm that no confidential container
number, route, customer, shipment event, or browser session is visible.
