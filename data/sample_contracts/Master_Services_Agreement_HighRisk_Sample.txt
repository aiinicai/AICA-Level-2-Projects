MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA)

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Apex Global India Tech Pvt. Ltd. ("Company") and Horizon Enterprise Solutions Ltd. ("Vendor").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Vendor shall provide cloud architecture consulting, software development, and technical support services as detailed in Statements of Work executed hereunder.

2. PAYMENT TERMS & DISPUTES
Payment for all validated invoices shall be disbursed by Buyer within ninety (90) days from the end of the calendar month in which the invoice is submitted. Buyer reserves the right to withhold payments during any dispute without incurring interest or statutory liabilities.

3. NON-COMPETE & RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS
For a period of twenty-four (24) months following termination of employment or engagement for any reason, the Individual/Contractor shall not directly or indirectly engage in, advise, or be employed by any business competing with the Company anywhere in the territory of India.

4. DATA PRIVACY & DATA TRANSFERS
The Vendor may freely process, transfer, or offshore all customer and employee personal data to any jurisdiction or affiliate without prior notice or consent. Vendor shall not be held liable for third-party breaches occurring on subcontractor servers.

5. CROSS-BORDER REMITTANCES
All payments shall be made directly in foreign currency to Overseas Vendor's offshore account without deduction of Indian withholding taxes or compliance with RBI Authorized Dealer approvals.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY & INDEMNIFICATION
Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Client against any and all claims, indirect damages, lost profits, or statutory penalties without any financial cap.

7. GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, USA, and disputes resolved exclusively in New York courts.
